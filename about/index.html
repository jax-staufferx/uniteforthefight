About

#UniteForTheFight. Different side. Same fight.

I make videos about this. This site is where they lead.

We spend our energy arguing with each other about abortion, guns and what we're allowed to say. Meanwhile the money that shapes who gets elected goes mostly unread. A video can get you angry for thirty seconds. I wanted something that lasts longer than that: the record, sourced, for your state, and something to do with it.

So here's who funds your senators and representative, what the people running for Senate this year have actually said, the numbers to call, and the protests near you. Not my opinion of them. Their words, their votes and their filings, with a link on every line so you can check me.

If you've stayed out of it because you don't like either side: staying out isn't neutral. Pick one thing on your state's page and do it.
