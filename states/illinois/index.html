Illinois Senate race

Open seat: Dick Durbin holds it and is not on the ballot. Candidates: the Democratic and Republican nominees, incumbent first.

Juliana Stratton

Democratic 98% on Kalshi

Lieutenant Governor of Illinois · Campaign website

On the record

  • IsraelSaid

    In an April 30, 2025 interview with Capitol News Illinois, Stratton declined to discuss her position on Israel's war with Hamas or whether Israel should agree to a ceasefire. Asked about foreign policy, she said instead that Washington needs a stable leader and that it does not have one with Donald Trump.

    "What people want, and need, is a stable leader in D.C."

    Stratton’s top priority in Senate campaign: Fighting back against Trump, NPR Illinois (Capitol News Illinois), April 30, 2025

  • ICESaid

    In a Jan. 26, 2026 Democratic primary debate hosted by the Chicago Sun-Times, WBEZ and the University of Chicago, Stratton criticized an opponent for voting for a resolution thanking ICE and said she wants to abolish the agency. On winning the primary in March 2026, she pledged to keep calling for abolishing ICE.

    "I want to abolish ICE"

    Lt. Gov. Stratton goes on attack in Sun-Times, WBEZ, UChicago debate against U.S. Reps. Kelly, Krishnamoorthi, NPR Illinois (Chicago Sun-Times/WBEZ), January 26, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In the same Jan. 26, 2026 debate, candidates were asked to name a Trump policy they support. Stratton said she could not name one.

    "there’s nothing that I’m going to say today that I can think of that I agree with Donald Trump on."

    Lt. Gov. Stratton goes on attack in Sun-Times, WBEZ, UChicago debate against U.S. Reps. Kelly, Krishnamoorthi, NPR Illinois (Chicago Sun-Times/WBEZ), January 26, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In her speech after winning the Democratic primary on March 17, 2026, Stratton said she would take her campaign's fight to President Trump, and pledged to keep calling for abolishing ICE and raising the minimum wage.

    "Courage powered this campaign, and courage will bring this fight straight to Donald Trump’s door."

    Juliana Stratton wins Democratic primary for U.S. Senate, NPR Illinois (Capitol News Illinois/Chicago Sun-Times), March 17, 2026

  • The economySaid

    Releasing her policy platform in late September 2025, Stratton blamed President Trump's tariffs and economic policies for higher prices and said the problem goes beyond his administration. The platform calls for a tax increase on people earning $1 million or more a year to pay for a middle-class tax cut.

    "Rising prices are just one symptom of a broken system. For too long, big corporations across every sector have operated virtually unchecked by Washington."

    Stratton aims to bring Pritzker administration policies to national stage, NPR Illinois (Capitol News Illinois), October 1, 2025

  • Workers and unionsSaid

    In the April 30, 2025 interview, Stratton pointed to Illinois raising its minimum wage to $15 and said the federal minimum wage, $7.25 since 2009, should also rise. Her campaign site now lists a $25 minimum wage among her priorities.

    "That has not happened on the federal level yet, and yet, people know that raising the minimum wage helps lift people up out of poverty."

    Stratton’s top priority in Senate campaign: Fighting back against Trump, NPR Illinois (Capitol News Illinois), April 30, 2025

  • AbortionSaid

    Her campaign's priorities page lists restoring the abortion protections of Roe v. Wade among her legislative priorities in the Senate. The page is undated.

    "Restoring the Abortion Protections of Roe v. Wade"

    Juliana's Priorities | Juliana Stratton, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

Don Tracy

Republican 2% on Kalshi

Campaign website

On the record

  • Israel

    No public statement found as of October 18, 2026.

  • ICESaid

    His campaign's priorities page says he supports stronger border security, enforcing immigration laws and opposing taxpayer-funded benefits for people in the country illegally. The page is undated.

    "Don supports stronger border security, enforcement of immigration laws, and an orderly legal immigration system."

    Defend Illinois | Don Tracy for U.S. Senate Campaign Priorities, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In a Capitol News Illinois interview reported July 8, 2026, Tracy defended the U.S. war with Iran, saying it was necessary to keep Iran from developing a nuclear weapon even though it has led to higher gas prices.

    "There is no gain without some pain"

    Illinois' U.S. Senate Republican nominee Don Tracy aims to slow cost-of-living, NPR Illinois (WGLT, citing Capitol News Illinois), July 8, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In the same interview, Tracy said he is not tying his campaign to President Trump, that he is "not a proxy for anybody," and that he differs with Trump on some issues and would decide issue by issue.

    "Some of those issues I do differ with the president. I think you'll find that I do, but I'll just take it on an issue-by-issue basis."

    Illinois' U.S. Senate Republican nominee Don Tracy aims to slow cost-of-living, NPR Illinois (WGLT, citing Capitol News Illinois), July 8, 2026

  • The economySaid

    In the same interview, Tracy said cost of living is the central issue of the election and named his three reasons for running.

    "I’m running to reduce the cost of living for all Illinoisans, especially working families"

    Illinois' U.S. Senate Republican nominee Don Tracy aims to slow cost-of-living, NPR Illinois (WGLT, citing Capitol News Illinois), July 8, 2026

  • EnergySaid

    In a Sept. 11, 2026 statement releasing a campaign ad, Tracy said Illinois policies supported by Lt. Gov. Juliana Stratton have pushed employers and power plants out of the state and raised electric bills.

    "You do not grow a state by incentivizing private employers and Illinoisans to move to other States and shutting down the power that allows communities to grow and prosper."

    Don Tracy Releases Fourth Ad: “Too Extreme”, Candidate's campaign site, September 11, 2026

  • TaxesSaid

    In a June 24, 2026 statement, after Illinois lawmakers delayed the state's scheduled automatic gas tax increase by six months, Tracy criticized the 2019 law that doubled the gas tax and tied it to inflation.

    "The fact that Gov. Pritzker and Lt. Gov. Juliana Stratton suddenly want to postpone their automatic gas tax increase is an admission that the policy is hurting Illinois families"

    Don Tracy: Election-Year Delay Doesn't Change Failed Gas Tax Policy, Candidate's campaign site, June 24, 2026

Your script

Hi, my name is ____, and I'm a constituent from ____, ZIP ____.

I'm calling about AIPAC money, ICE and checks and balances.

I want my senator to refuse money from AIPAC PAC and United Democracy Project, and to say so publicly.

I want my senator to vote against more money for ICE detention and enforcement.

I want my senator to use Congress's oversight power, including hearings, subpoenas and votes, whichever party is in charge.

Please record my position. Thank you for your time.

Some offices log one issue per call. If they do, call back for the rest.

Call

    Tips for calling
    • Call your own senators and representative. Offices mostly count calls from their own voters.
    • Say your ZIP so the call counts as a constituent.
    • You'll talk to a staff member, not the official. That's normal: they log your call.
    • One or two issues per call is best.
    • Voicemail counts. Offices go through it every day.
    • If the D.C. office is busy, try a state office, or the U.S. Capitol Switchboard at (202) 224-3121.
    • Call again next week.
