Republican Incumbent98% on Kalshi

On the record

  • IsraelVoted

    On April 15, 2026, Capito voted against discharging S.J.Res. 32 from the Foreign Relations Committee, a joint resolution to block a proposed foreign military sale of defense articles and services to the Government of Israel. The motion failed 40-59.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate: Roll Call Vote 119th Congress - 2nd Session, Vote Number 80, U.S. Senate, April 15, 2026

  • ICESaid

    On June 5, 2026, after the Senate passed the Secure America Act, which funds ICE and Customs and Border Protection through the rest of the Trump administration, Capito said in a statement that she was proud to support it.

    "Republicans delivered even stronger support for ICE and CBP, and I was proud to support this effort"

    Capito Votes to Fund ICE and CBP, U.S. Senate (office of Sen. Shelley Moore Capito), June 5, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In a statement after President Trump's State of the Union address on February 24, 2026, Capito said the address highlighted progress made during his first year in office.

    "President Trump delivered a forward-looking message highlighting the incredible progress achieved during his first year in office"

    Capito Statement on President Trump's State of the Union Address, U.S. Senate (office of Sen. Shelley Moore Capito), February 24, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    Closing the same February 24, 2026 State of the Union statement, Capito said she looks forward to continuing to work with President Trump.

    "I look forward to continuing to work with President Trump to deliver real solutions and create a brighter future for West Virginians and all Americans."

    Capito Statement on President Trump's State of the Union Address, U.S. Senate (office of Sen. Shelley Moore Capito), February 24, 2026

  • TaxesVoted

    On July 1, 2025, Capito voted for Senate passage of H.R. 1, the budget reconciliation bill of tax and spending changes known as the One Big Beautiful Bill. It passed 50-50, with the Vice President voting to break the tie.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate: Roll Call Vote 119th Congress - 1st Session, Vote Number 372, U.S. Senate, July 1, 2025

  • EnergySaid

    On September 14, 2026, as chair of the Environment and Public Works Committee, Capito praised the EPA's repeal of the Biden administration's power plant rule known as the Clean Power Plan 2.0, which she said would have eliminated coal and natural gas power generation.

    "I have long opposed this rule and warned of the devastating economic impacts it would have in West Virginia"

    Chairman Capito Applauds Trump EPA Repeal of Clean Power Plan 2.0, U.S. Senate (office of Sen. Shelley Moore Capito), September 14, 2026

  • Democracy and electionsSaid

    In a Senate floor speech on March 19, 2026, Capito backed the SAVE America Act, which would require photo ID to vote and proof of citizenship to register.

    "Americans deserve to have confidence that every vote that is cast and counted comes from an American citizen"

    VIDEO: Capito Delivers Speech on the SAVE America Act and DHS Shutdown, U.S. Senate (office of Sen. Shelley Moore Capito), March 19, 2026

  • AbortionSaid

    In January 2025, Capito joined a letter from 36 senators and 108 House members asking President Trump to reinstate and broaden the anti-abortion ("pro-life") policies of his first term.

    "We look forward to the reinstatement of Trump policies on life in the first days of your second term"

    Capito, Colleagues Encourage President Trump to Reinstate Life-Affirming Policies, U.S. Senate (office of Sen. Shelley Moore Capito), January 22, 2025

On the record

  • IsraelSaid

    Fetty Anderson's campaign issues page says she supports a two-state solution, that both Israel and Palestine deserve to exist, and that she would not vote to send or sell weapons to any country except for self-defense. The page is undated.

    "I would not vote to send or sell weapons to any country except for purely self-defense."

    Issues | Rachel Fetty Anderson for U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • ICE

    No public statement found as of October 18, 2026.

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    On the Social Security section of her campaign issues page, Fetty Anderson says the current administration does not respect taxpayers' money or citizens' well-being. The page is undated; it cites 2026 Social Security figures.

    "Every day, our current administration reflects a lack of respect for our tax dollars and a lack of regard for our citizens’ well-being."

    Issues | Rachel Fetty Anderson for U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In a West Virginia Watch interview published before the May 2026 primary, Fetty Anderson said she wants Congress to restore checks and balances on the executive branch, and to fight what she called a growing movement toward "outright fascism" in Congress and U.S. politics.

    "outright fascism"

    Eclectic group of WV Dems are competing in primary for a chance to flip Capito’s US Senate seat, West Virginia Watch (republished by West Virginia Daily News), May 6, 2026

  • Health careSaid

    Speaking to campaign volunteers in Charleston in September 2026, Fetty Anderson criticized cuts to Medicaid and the Affordable Care Act; The Dominion Post reported that she supports single-payer health care and rolling back the Medicaid and ACA changes made by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act.

    "We just cannot handle cuts in Medicaid. We cannot handle people getting kicked off the ACA"

    Come rain or shine: Anderson campaign highlights discontent with Capito’s record, The Dominion Post, September 26, 2026

  • Money in politicsSaid

    At the same September 2026 Charleston meeting, Fetty Anderson told volunteers her campaign takes no corporate, industry or foreign-government-aligned money; the paper reported she has sworn off PAC donations and money from groups advocating for foreign nations, such as the American Israel Public Affairs Committee.

    "The bold move that we made was to run without corporate financing and industry financing and funding from any group that is supporting a foreign government."

    Come rain or shine: Anderson campaign highlights discontent with Capito’s record, The Dominion Post, September 26, 2026

  • Workers and unionsSaid

    In September 2026, Fetty Anderson tied West Virginia's low wages to right-to-work laws and the weakening of unions, and said she supports strengthening labor unions.

    "I think at the heart of our problem is that our wages are too low, and that’s because of union suppression"

    Come rain or shine: Anderson campaign highlights discontent with Capito’s record, The Dominion Post, September 26, 2026

  • TechnologySaid

    In September 2026, Fetty Anderson said she opposes fast-tracking data center development in a way that removes county authority, and that it needs to be stopped before more centers are built.

    "We have to stop that right now because once these data centers are built, they are there."

    Come rain or shine: Anderson campaign highlights discontent with Capito’s record, The Dominion Post, September 26, 2026

  • AbortionSaid

    On MetroNews "Talkline" after winning the May 2026 Democratic primary, Fetty Anderson, who supports abortion rights, spoke about abortion as part of a call for full equality for women.

    "The issue of abortion—that is one medical procedure that some women need or desire"

    Former Morgantown elected official to face Capito in the fall, WAJR, May 14, 2026

Your script

Hi, my name is ____, and I'm a constituent from ____, ZIP ____.

I'm calling about AIPAC money, ICE and checks and balances.

I want my senator to refuse money from AIPAC PAC and United Democracy Project, and to say so publicly.

I want my senator to vote against more money for ICE detention and enforcement.

I want my senator to use Congress's oversight power, including hearings, subpoenas and votes, whichever party is in charge.

Please record my position. Thank you for your time.

Some offices log one issue per call. If they do, call back for the rest.

Call

    Tips for calling
    • Call your own senators and representative. Offices mostly count calls from their own voters.
    • Say your ZIP so the call counts as a constituent.
    • You'll talk to a staff member, not the official. That's normal: they log your call.
    • One or two issues per call is best.
    • Voicemail counts. Offices go through it every day.
    • If the D.C. office is busy, try a state office, or the U.S. Capitol Switchboard at (202) 224-3121.
    • Call again next week.
