New Jersey Senate race

Cory Booker holds the seat and is running again. Candidates: the Democratic and Republican nominees, incumbent first.

Cory Booker

Democratic Incumbent97% on Kalshi

U.S. senator, running for re-election · Campaign website

On the record

  • IsraelSaid

    In an April 15, 2026 statement, issued the day he voted for two Senate resolutions to block proposed arms sales to Israel, Booker said he opposes more weapons for the Iran war, including to allies. He also said he will always support Israel's defense and deterrence capabilities.

    "I am using every tool available to me to stop this war, and do not support more weapons to continue the combat, whether it is to our own government or to one of our closest allies."

    Booker Statement on Joint Resolutions of Disapproval, U.S. Senate (office of Sen. Cory Booker), April 15, 2026

  • ICESaid

    In an Oct. 9, 2026 statement about a video that appeared to show ICE agents forcibly taking a man away from two children in Harrison, N.J., Booker said his office was opening an inquiry and that he would not vote for more ICE funding.

    "I will not support another penny for a reckless and out of control ICE"

    Booker Statement Regarding ICE Incident on Harrison Avenue, U.S. Senate (office of Sen. Cory Booker), October 9, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    Introducing the Presidential Pardon Integrity Act on Oct. 16, 2026, Booker said President Trump has used the pardon power to reward political allies and donors, and that the bill would require disclosure from those who lobby for pardons.

    "This President claims to punish the ‘worst of the worst’ yet pardons people convicted of serious crimes because they are his political allies, large campaign donors, or have corporate and financial ties to him and his family."

    Booker Introduces Bill to Combat Trump’s Pardons for Sale Scheme, U.S. Senate (office of Sen. Cory Booker), October 16, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In a June 23, 2026 statement after the Senate passed a war powers resolution to end the U.S. war with Iran, Booker said Trump, Hegseth and Vance had tried to "outrun the Constitution" and that the Senate had said no, and called on people to keep pushing back.

    "The power of the people is greater than the people in power. We have much more work to do. Keep standing up"

    Booker, Senators Praise Senate Passage of Resolution to End Iran War, U.S. Senate (office of Sen. Cory Booker), June 23, 2026

  • Foreign policySaid

    In an Oct. 8, 2026 debate with Republican Justin Murphy, Booker said he would push to end the Iran war and that military force should be a last resort after diplomacy, and compared the war to past costly U.S. wars in the Middle East.

    "This is the kind of yahoo cowboy foreign policy that has mired us in Middle Eastern war after Middle Eastern war."

    Cory Booker, Justin Murphy split on Iran war, economy during US Senate debate, New Jersey Monitor, October 8, 2026

  • The economySaid

    In the Oct. 8, 2026 debate, Booker said the Iran war and President Trump's tariffs have raised costs for New Jersey families, including health care and fuel costs for small businesses.

    "Costs across the board have gone up, as I said, between $4,000 and $5,000 for the average family"

    Cory Booker, Justin Murphy split on Iran war, economy during US Senate debate, New Jersey Monitor, October 8, 2026

Campaign website

On the record

  • IsraelSaid

    The national security section of his 2026 campaign platform cites the October 7, 2023 attack as showing that Islamist terrorism remains a threat. The platform does not mention Israel, US aid to Israel or the war in Gaza by name.

    "radical islamic terrorism is still a threat to civilized nations"

    MURPHY FOR U.S. SENATE 2026 - New Jersey, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • ICESaid

    His campaign platform supports Section 287(g) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, which lets state and local police take part in immigration enforcement. It also backs withholding federal aid from sanctuary jurisdictions.

    "I support section 287 (g) of the INA (Immigration & Nationality Act) to permit state and local law enforcement involvement in immigration enforcement."

    MURPHY FOR U.S. SENATE 2026 - New Jersey, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    On the Trump administration's record, his campaign platform says President Trump has secured the southern border. It also calls for finishing the border wall started under the Trump administration.

    "Thankfully, President Trump has secured America’s southern border."

    MURPHY FOR U.S. SENATE 2026 - New Jersey, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    His campaign platform says the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE), set up under the Trump administration, should be made permanent. It also backs a Balanced Budget Amendment.

    "DOGE should become a permanent federal endeavor."

    MURPHY FOR U.S. SENATE 2026 - New Jersey, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • TaxesSaid

    His campaign platform calls for abolishing the IRS and the Internal Revenue Code and replacing them with a flat income tax of 10 to 12 percent. It also calls for ending the capital gains tax, the estate tax and the alternative minimum tax.

    "I advocate abolishing the Internal Revenue Code and enacting a Flat-Tax rate of 10% – 12%"

    MURPHY FOR U.S. SENATE 2026 - New Jersey, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • AbortionSaid

    His campaign platform says he is pro-life and believes life begins at conception. He lists exceptions for the life of the mother, rape and incest, and opposes tax dollars funding abortion.

    "I am pro-life; I believe life begins at conception, and there is inherent value in each individual life."

    MURPHY FOR U.S. SENATE 2026 - New Jersey, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • GunsSaid

    His campaign platform says he will oppose government efforts to restrict the right to bear arms, and that he supports constitutional carry.

    "I will oppose any attempt by the government that threatens to take away our Constitutional right to bear arms and protect ourselves, our families, and our property."

    MURPHY FOR U.S. SENATE 2026 - New Jersey, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • EducationSaid

    His campaign platform calls for abolishing the federal Department of Education and splitting its budget among local school boards for public schools and voucher programs. It also supports school choice.

    "I advocate the complete abolition of the Federal Department of Education"

    MURPHY FOR U.S. SENATE 2026 - New Jersey, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • Health careSaid

    His campaign platform says he supports universal coverage but opposes government-run health care. He proposes a Health Savings Account for every American, buying insurance across state lines, and a Medicare and Medicaid fraud unit.

    "I agree with those who want universal health coverage for Americans, however, I adamantly oppose government-run healthcare."

    MURPHY FOR U.S. SENATE 2026 - New Jersey, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • Foreign policySaid

    His campaign's China issue page calls the Chinese government the most serious nation-state threat to the US. It proposes tariffs on Chinese-made goods, expelling Chinese diplomats, ending ambiguity about defending Taiwan, and continuing to arm Taiwan and US allies in Asia.

    "remove ambiguity as it relates to the defense of Taiwan; continue to arm Taiwan and our Asian allies Japan, South Korea, and the Philippines"

    Issue China - Justin for U.S. Senate 2026 - New Jersey, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

Your script

Hi, my name is ____, and I'm a constituent from ____, ZIP ____.

I'm calling about AIPAC money, ICE and checks and balances.

I want my senator to refuse money from AIPAC PAC and United Democracy Project, and to say so publicly.

I want my senator to vote against more money for ICE detention and enforcement.

I want my senator to use Congress's oversight power, including hearings, subpoenas and votes, whichever party is in charge.

Please record my position. Thank you for your time.

Some offices log one issue per call. If they do, call back for the rest.

Call

    Tips for calling
    • Call your own senators and representative. Offices mostly count calls from their own voters.
    • Say your ZIP so the call counts as a constituent.
    • You'll talk to a staff member, not the official. That's normal: they log your call.
    • One or two issues per call is best.
    • Voicemail counts. Offices go through it every day.
    • If the D.C. office is busy, try a state office, or the U.S. Capitol Switchboard at (202) 224-3121.
    • Call again next week.
