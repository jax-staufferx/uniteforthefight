On the record

  • IsraelVoted

    On April 15, 2026, Cotton voted no on a motion to discharge S.J.Res. 32, a joint resolution disapproving a proposed U.S. foreign military sale of defense articles and services to the Government of Israel. The motion failed 40-59.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate Roll Call Votes 119th Congress - 2nd Session (Vote Number 80), U.S. Senate, April 15, 2026

  • ICESaid

    On April 30, 2026, after the House voted to fund the Department of Homeland Security and end a 76-day lapse in its funding, Cotton said Democrats had sought to defund ICE and the Border Patrol and that Republicans would increase their funding.

    "Republicans will now supercharge ICE and border patrol funding without a single Democrat vote."

    Cotton Statement Following House Vote on Department of Homeland Security Funding, Office of Sen. Tom Cotton (senate.gov), April 30, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In a Sept. 9, 2025 statement on crime and contraband cellphones in prisons, Cotton said he supports President Trump's law-and-order efforts and praised the FCC's move to let prisons jam contraband phones.

    "I applaud the Trump administration’s effort to crack down on crime"

    President Trump and Republicans are Restoring Law and Order, Office of Sen. Tom Cotton (senate.gov), September 9, 2025

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In a March 18, 2026 Senate floor speech opposing a war powers resolution on Iran, Cotton responded to critics who say the Iran war departs from Trump's "America First" promises, saying Trump has been consistent that Iran cannot have a nuclear weapon.

    "Some critics claim that the president has abandoned his principles and promises to put America first. I disagree."

    Floor Speech Opposing War Powers Resolution, Office of Sen. Tom Cotton (senate.gov), March 18, 2026

  • Foreign policyVoted

    On Sept. 24, 2026, Cotton voted no on H.Con.Res. 89, a concurrent resolution directing the President under the War Powers Resolution to remove U.S. armed forces from hostilities with Iran. It was rejected 49-50.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate Roll Call Votes 119th Congress - 2nd Session (Vote Number 244), U.S. Senate, September 24, 2026

  • EnergySaid

    On Sept. 22, 2026, Cotton introduced the Supply Relief and Domestic Production Cost Reduction Act, which during fuel-supply shortages would cap onshore oil and gas royalties at 8%, cut lease and permitting costs, and add tax incentives for domestic production.

    "Arkansans are rightfully feeling pressure at the pump. My bill would lower prices by cutting red tape and boosting American energy production"

    Cotton Introduces Bill to Lower Gas Prices for Arkansans, Office of Sen. Tom Cotton (senate.gov), September 22, 2026

  • TaxesVoted

    On July 1, 2025, Cotton voted yes on Senate passage of H.R. 1, the budget reconciliation bill known as the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, which extended the 2017 tax cuts. The vote was 50-50, and the Vice President voted yea to pass it.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate Roll Call Votes 119th Congress - 1st Session (Vote Number 372), U.S. Senate, July 1, 2025

Hallie Shoffner

Democratic 5% on Kalshi

Campaign website

On the record

  • Israel

    No public statement found as of October 18, 2026.

  • ICESaid

    Asked in February 2026 about immigration enforcement after federal agents killed Renee Good and Alex Pretti in Minnesota, Shoffner said the agents' actions were further dividing the nation and that the current approach is not the immigration reform people want. She also said federal money should go to law enforcement that reduces crime and is held to accountability standards.

    "I think that everyone believes we should have immigration reform, but that everyone also believes that this isn’t it"

    Issue Spotlight: Where Arkansas' U.S. Senate candidates stand on immigration policy, Arkansas Advocate, February 26, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    Responding on June 26, 2026 to President Trump's executive order on regenerative agriculture and the administration's request for $11 billion in farm aid, Shoffner credited Trump for the order, while warning that farm aid sent through the current system ends up with large agricultural suppliers rather than farmers.

    "President Trump deserves credit for signing this Executive Order."

    Hallie Shoffner on Trump's Regenerative Agriculture Executive Order and Proposed $11 Billion Farm Aid Fund: "Better Late Than Never — But Tom Cotton Is Nowhere to Be Found", Candidate's campaign site, June 26, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In a February 2026 interview before the Democratic primary, Shoffner said she was not asking voters to become Democrats, only to agree that things could be done much better than they are now.

    "I’m just asking that you think we can do a hell of a lot better than what we’re doing right now."

    Republican Sen. Tom Cotton focus of GOP, Democratic primaries, Arkansas Advocate, February 17, 2026

  • Foreign policySaid

    In April 2026, Shoffner said she would have supported Congress's effort to limit Trump's war powers on Iran and that spending billions of dollars a week on the war is unpopular with Arkansans she has met.

    "There is not the appetite for an expensive, prolonged foreign conflict because we have real problems right here at home"

    Issue Spotlight: Where Arkansas' U.S. Senate candidates stand on the Iran war, Arkansas Advocate, April 7, 2026

  • FarmingSaid

    After the Senate Agriculture Committee voted 12-11 in September 2026 to advance the Farm Bill, Shoffner called on the full Senate to pass it before the Sept. 30 extension deadline, while saying she shares Democrats' concerns about new SNAP cost-sharing requirements for states.

    "I would have voted to advance this bill."

    Shoffner Calls for Senate Vote on Farm Bill Before September 30 Deadline, Candidate's campaign site, September 17, 2026

  • The economySaid

    Launching her campaign in July 2025, Shoffner criticized Cotton's support for the 2025 tax law and listed her own economic priorities.

    "I’ll cut taxes for blue-collar workers, fight to restore Medicaid, save Social Security, cut the deficit, and be radically focused on bringing costs down for regular people"

    Arkansas farmer challenges Cotton for U.S. Senate seat, Arkansas Advocate, July 15, 2025

Your script

Hi, my name is ____, and I'm a constituent from ____, ZIP ____.

I'm calling about AIPAC money, ICE and checks and balances.

I want my senator to refuse money from AIPAC PAC and United Democracy Project, and to say so publicly.

I want my senator to vote against more money for ICE detention and enforcement.

I want my senator to use Congress's oversight power, including hearings, subpoenas and votes, whichever party is in charge.

Please record my position. Thank you for your time.

Some offices log one issue per call. If they do, call back for the rest.

Call

    Tips for calling
    • Call your own senators and representative. Offices mostly count calls from their own voters.
    • Say your ZIP so the call counts as a constituent.
    • You'll talk to a staff member, not the official. That's normal: they log your call.
    • One or two issues per call is best.
    • Voicemail counts. Offices go through it every day.
    • If the D.C. office is busy, try a state office, or the U.S. Capitol Switchboard at (202) 224-3121.
    • Call again next week.
