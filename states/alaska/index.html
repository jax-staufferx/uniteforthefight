Alaska Senate race

Dan Sullivan holds the seat and is running again. Candidates: the Democratic and Republican nominees, incumbent first.

Dan Sullivan

Republican Incumbent26% on Kalshi

U.S. senator, running for re-election · Campaign website · On the ballot as Sullivan, Dan S.

On the record

  • IsraelVoted

    On April 15, 2026, Sullivan voted no on a motion to discharge S.J.Res. 32, a joint resolution disapproving a proposed U.S. foreign military sale of defense articles and services to the Government of Israel. The motion failed 40-59.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate Roll Call Votes 119th Congress - 2nd Session (Vote Number 80), U.S. Senate, April 15, 2026

  • ICESaid

    In a Jan. 28, 2026 statement after a Border Patrol agent fatally shot Alex Pretti in Minneapolis, Sullivan called for a full investigation, said he supports law enforcement and the removal of immigrants with criminal records, and said ICE must change its tactics and procedures.

    "At the same time, ICE must learn from the tragic, violent confrontations with protesters in recent weeks and adjust its tactics and procedures to prevent future loss of civilian life when carrying out these missions"

    Sullivan calls for full investigation into federal immigration tactics in Minneapolis, Alaska's News Source (KTUU), January 28, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    Asked by a state senator during his Feb. 18, 2026 address to the Alaska Legislature whether he would say no to Trump, Sullivan said his guide in dealing with the administration is what is good for Alaska and the country, that he sometimes criticizes it in public, and he named federal funding freezes and worker layoffs as areas of disagreement.

    "When they do things that I don't like, there's times that I will beat them up in public."

    Sen. Sullivan defends his approach to the Trump administration in address to Alaska Legislature, Anchorage Daily News, February 18, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In his Feb. 18, 2026 address to the Alaska Legislature, Sullivan described an "Alaska comeback" following the change from the Biden administration to the Trump administration, citing new North Slope oil and gas access and federal support for a natural gas pipeline.

    "We're now beginning to see the beginnings of a real comeback and real progress on goals we've dreamed about collectively for decades"

    In legislative speech, Dan Sullivan reiterates support for Trump administration, denounces Democrats, Alaska Beacon, February 18, 2026

  • Foreign policyVoted

    On Sept. 24, 2026, Sullivan voted no on H.Con.Res. 89, a concurrent resolution directing the President under the War Powers Resolution to remove U.S. armed forces from hostilities with Iran. It was rejected 49-50.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate Roll Call Votes 119th Congress - 2nd Session (Vote Number 244), U.S. Senate, September 24, 2026

  • EnergySaid

    At an Oct. 13, 2026 campaign rally in Wasilla with President Trump, Sullivan said the best way to lower Alaska's fuel costs is to produce more oil and gas in the state.

    "It's important to remember this: the best way to address Alaska's energy challenges is to produce more Alaskan energy by and for Alaskans"

    In Alaska campaign rally, Trump and Sullivan say Iran war is worth the cost to Americans, Alaska Public Media (Alaska Beacon), October 13, 2026

  • Health careSaid

    In the Oct. 15, 2026 televised Senate debate, Sullivan said the 2025 One Big Beautiful Bill Act did not cut Medicaid funding for Alaskans and said he had voted twice to extend the Affordable Care Act premium tax credits.

    "I actually bucked my party and voted to extend the premium tax credits on Obamacare twice"

    Sullivan and Peltola lob personal attacks as they clash over Iran war and the economy, Anchorage Daily News, October 15, 2026

  • Social Security and MedicareSaid

    In the Oct. 15, 2026 debate, Sullivan said he never voted to raise the retirement age and that people receiving Social Security should keep getting their current benefits.

    "we need to make sure it's a lockbox. They're going to get what they continue to have. You can't touch it"

    Alaska's final U.S. Senate debate devolves into mudslinging between Peltola, Sullivan, Alaska Beacon, October 15, 2026

  • Democracy and electionsSaid

    Speaking to reporters on Feb. 18, 2026, Sullivan defended his support for the SAVE Act, which would require proof of citizenship to vote. He said he does not believe voter fraud is widespread in Alaska but that it is elsewhere.

    "I think having an ID requirement that makes sure that the people who are voting in our country are Americans is not unreasonable"

    Sen. Sullivan defends his approach to the Trump administration in address to Alaska Legislature, Anchorage Daily News, February 18, 2026

Mary Peltola

Democratic 75% on Kalshi

Campaign website · On the ballot as Peltola, Mary

On the record

  • IsraelVoted

    As Alaska's U.S. Representative, Peltola voted yes on April 20, 2024 on House passage of H.R. 8034, the Israel Security Supplemental Appropriations Act, 2024.

    Roll call: Office of the Clerk, U.S. House of Representatives - Roll Call 152 (H.R. 8034), Office of the Clerk, U.S. House of Representatives, April 20, 2024

  • ICESaid

    In an Oct. 11, 2026 op-ed published by the Anchorage Daily News and reposted on her campaign site, Peltola wrote that a secure border keeps Alaska communities safe and that she would work in the Senate to secure the border. The op-ed addresses border security; it does not discuss ICE operations directly.

    "A secure border is part of keeping Alaska communities safe too, which is why I’ve always stood up to my own party — including President Biden and his border czar — to secure the border and fund the local law enforcement our communities depend on."

    OP-ED: Mary Peltola Will Be An Independent Voice for Alaskans, Candidate's campaign site, October 11, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    Speaking to reporters after a May 14, 2026 rally in Juneau, Peltola said she supports Congress limiting the Iran war and would support a war powers resolution, and said Congress and the Supreme Court should check the Trump administration.

    "Clearly, there is not a balance of power right now. Clearly this administration has an outsized role in the direction, whether it’s tariffs or wars."

    Alaska U.S. Senate candidate Mary Peltola calls for congressional term limits, Alaska Beacon, May 14, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    Speaking about the Iran war at an Anchorage forum on Oct. 8, 2026, Peltola said Congress should stand up to the president on wars.

    "We should be standing up to the president, and there have been many times where the Senate and the House have said no to the administration on wars. Where is our backbone?"

    In Alaska campaign rally, Trump and Sullivan say Iran war is worth the cost to Americans, Alaska Public Media (Alaska Beacon), October 8, 2026

  • Foreign policySaid

    In the Oct. 15, 2026 televised Senate debate, Peltola said peace with Iran is not in sight and that U.S. forces should be withdrawn from the conflict.

    "There does not seem to be a peace deal on the horizon, and I think it’s so important that our military be removed and protected"

    Alaska's final U.S. Senate debate devolves into mudslinging between Peltola, Sullivan, Alaska Beacon, October 15, 2026

  • Social Security and MedicareSaid

    Answering a question on Social Security in the Oct. 15, 2026 debate, Peltola said she would work to eliminate federal taxes on Social Security benefits.

    "I will always fight for our seniors’ hard-earned benefits, and in the Senate, I’ll fight to eliminate taxes on Social Security benefits."

    Alaska's final U.S. Senate debate devolves into mudslinging between Peltola, Sullivan, Alaska Beacon, October 15, 2026

  • Money in politicsSaid

    At a May 14, 2026 rally at the Alaska State Capitol in Juneau, Peltola called for congressional term limits (12 years in the House and 12 in the Senate), a ban on insider trading by members of Congress, and stronger campaign finance rules.

    "We’ve got to enact term limits. We’ve got to stop insider trading. We’ve got to stop the ability for people to line their own pockets."

    Alaska U.S. Senate candidate Mary Peltola calls for congressional term limits, Alaska Beacon, May 14, 2026

  • EnergySaid

    In her Oct. 11, 2026 op-ed, Peltola wrote that she supports expanding Alaska oil and gas development, refining more Alaska oil in-state, and the Alaska LNG pipeline.

    "In the Senate, I will always fight for Alaskans’ right to develop our own resources by Alaskans, for Alaskans."

    OP-ED: Mary Peltola Will Be An Independent Voice for Alaskans, Candidate's campaign site, October 11, 2026

Your script

Hi, my name is ____, and I'm a constituent from ____, ZIP ____.

I'm calling about AIPAC money, ICE and checks and balances.

I want my senator to refuse money from AIPAC PAC and United Democracy Project, and to say so publicly.

I want my senator to vote against more money for ICE detention and enforcement.

I want my senator to use Congress's oversight power, including hearings, subpoenas and votes, whichever party is in charge.

Please record my position. Thank you for your time.

Some offices log one issue per call. If they do, call back for the rest.

Call

    Tips for calling
    • Call your own senators and representative. Offices mostly count calls from their own voters.
    • Say your ZIP so the call counts as a constituent.
    • You'll talk to a staff member, not the official. That's normal: they log your call.
    • One or two issues per call is best.
    • Voicemail counts. Offices go through it every day.
    • If the D.C. office is busy, try a state office, or the U.S. Capitol Switchboard at (202) 224-3121.
    • Call again next week.
