Mark Warner

Democratic Incumbent98% on Kalshi

U.S. senator, running for re-election · Campaign website · On the ballot as Mark R. Warner

On the record

  • IsraelVoted

    On September 29, 2026, Warner voted yes on a motion to discharge S.Res. 852, a resolution requesting information on Israel's human rights practices under section 502B(c) of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961. The motion failed, 47 to 51.

    Roll call: Roll Call Vote 119th Congress - 2nd Session, Vote Number 252, U.S. Senate, September 29, 2026

  • ICEVoted

    On June 4, 2026, during debate on the S. 2 reconciliation bill, Warner voted yes on a motion to waive budget rules for Sen. Baldwin's amendment to redirect funding for U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement to the Child Care and Development Block Grant program. The motion failed, 46 to 53.

    Roll call: Roll Call Vote 119th Congress - 2nd Session, Vote Number 149, U.S. Senate, June 4, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In an October 2026 CBS 6 (WTVR) candidate interview, Warner said that when starting a war of choice with Iran a president should bring allies along and that President Trump did not, and said the president has angered most of America's friends and allies.

    "Unfortunately, again, this president has angered most of those friends and allies."

    Sen. Mark Warner talks inflation, Iran war and AI as he seeks Senate re-election, WTVR CBS 6 (Richmond), October 12, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In the same October 2026 CBS 6 interview, Warner said bringing down costs requires ending the war with Iran and getting rid of the current tariff policies.

    "Trying to bring down costs, in my mind, means we've got to end this war of choice with Iran"

    Sen. Mark Warner talks inflation, Iran war and AI as he seeks Senate re-election, WTVR CBS 6 (Richmond), October 12, 2026

  • Foreign policyVoted

    On July 30, 2026, Warner voted yes on a motion to discharge S.J.Res. 181, a joint resolution to direct the removal of U.S. armed forces from hostilities within or against Iran that have not been authorized by Congress. The motion failed, 49 to 50.

    Roll call: Roll Call Vote 119th Congress - 2nd Session, Vote Number 216, U.S. Senate, July 30, 2026

  • Trade and tariffsVoted

    On October 30, 2025, Warner voted yes on S.J.Res. 88, a joint resolution terminating the national emergency declared to impose global tariffs. It passed the Senate 51 to 47.

    Roll call: Roll Call Vote 119th Congress - 1st Session, Vote Number 600, U.S. Senate, October 30, 2025

  • TaxesVoted

    On July 1, 2025, Warner voted no on final Senate passage of H.R. 1, the 2025 reconciliation bill of tax and spending changes. The vote was 50 to 50, and the vice president voted yes to pass it.

    Roll call: Roll Call Vote 119th Congress - 1st Session, Vote Number 372, U.S. Senate, July 1, 2025

  • Health careSaid

    In the October 2026 CBS 6 interview, Warner said he now supports moving to universal health care, a position he said he did not hold 10 years ago.

    "I think we need to move to universal healthcare so we can actually focus on cost containment"

    Sen. Mark Warner talks inflation, Iran war and AI as he seeks Senate re-election, WTVR CBS 6 (Richmond), October 12, 2026

  • TechnologySaid

    In the October 2026 CBS 6 interview, Warner said data centers should meet a minimum federal standard, including bringing their own clean power and setbacks from communities, or lose a federal tax benefit.

    "I think there will be data centers because AI is not going away. But there needs to be a minimum federal standard."

    Sen. Mark Warner talks inflation, Iran war and AI as he seeks Senate re-election, WTVR CBS 6 (Richmond), October 12, 2026

    No public statement found as of October 18, 2026.

  • Foreign policySaid

    In the same September 2026 Cardinal News interview, Mizusawa said he feels the pain of higher gas prices during the Iran war but called it a small price compared with what could otherwise happen to the country.

    "I think this is a relatively small price to pay for something worse that could happen to our country."

    Virginia's Senate race is taking place outside the national spotlight, Cardinal News, September 22, 2026

  • The economySaid

    In a September 2026 candidate questionnaire for WAVY-TV, Mizusawa said the national debt has more than tripled since Warner joined the Senate and that he would work to identify waste and cut excessive federal spending.

    "In the Senate, I will take a careful approach to curb excessive spending and make government smaller, more efficient, and more accountable to taxpayers."

    Candidate Profile: Bert Mizusawa (U.S. Senate, Virginia), WAVY-TV 10 (Norfolk), September 10, 2026

  • Health careSaid

    Asked by WRIC in September 2026 how he would make health care more affordable, Mizusawa said he wants to rein in costs tied to the ACA and Medicaid expansion and keep program benefits from going to insurance companies.

    "I wanna make sure that the benefits of these health programs go to the people of Virginia and not to the insurance companies."

    Warner unveils new proposed healthcare legislation, Mizusawa responds, WRIC ABC 8News (Richmond), September 24, 2026

  • Money in politicsSaid

    Asked in the September 2026 WAVY-TV questionnaire whether campaign contributions influence how he would vote, Mizusawa answered no.

    "Campaign contributions will never determine how I vote or cause me to support a policy that I do not believe is right for Virginia and the country."

    Candidate Profile: Bert Mizusawa (U.S. Senate, Virginia), WAVY-TV 10 (Norfolk), September 10, 2026

  • TechnologySaid

    In the September 2026 Cardinal News interview, Mizusawa said data centers can be placed in remote areas and should not be built where local communities do not want them, while warning that regulation should not let the industry fall behind China.

    "Data centers should not be put in any local community that doesn't want it."

    Virginia's Senate race is taking place outside the national spotlight, Cardinal News, September 22, 2026

  • TaxesSaid

    In a June 2026 Cardinal News interview during the Republican primary, Mizusawa said he wants to streamline the tax code.

    "I would like to make it simpler to file taxes."

    Three Republicans seek U.S. Senate nomination to face Warner; all cite ties to Trump, Cardinal News, June 19, 2026

Your script

Hi, my name is ____, and I'm a constituent from ____, ZIP ____.

I'm calling about AIPAC money, ICE and checks and balances.

I want my senator to refuse money from AIPAC PAC and United Democracy Project, and to say so publicly.

I want my senator to vote against more money for ICE detention and enforcement.

I want my senator to use Congress's oversight power, including hearings, subpoenas and votes, whichever party is in charge.

Please record my position. Thank you for your time.

Some offices log one issue per call. If they do, call back for the rest.

Call

    Tips for calling
    • Call your own senators and representative. Offices mostly count calls from their own voters.
    • Say your ZIP so the call counts as a constituent.
    • You'll talk to a staff member, not the official. That's normal: they log your call.
    • One or two issues per call is best.
    • Voicemail counts. Offices go through it every day.
    • If the D.C. office is busy, try a state office, or the U.S. Capitol Switchboard at (202) 224-3121.
    • Call again next week.
