Andy Barr

Republican 97% on Kalshi

U.S. Representative for Kentucky's 6th district · Campaign website

On the record

  • IsraelSaid

    In a campaign statement on 2025-06-16, during Israel's strikes on Iran, Barr praised President Trump's support for Israel and called on Congress to back Israel's military operations against Iran's nuclear program.

    "I applaud President Trump for standing with Israel at this critical time, and as a member of the House Foreign Affairs Committee, I call on my colleagues in Congress to support Israeli military operations as long as it takes to eliminate the nuclear threat from Iran."

    Barr Blasts KY GOP Senate Opponents for Silence on Israel, Pledges to Stand with Trump, Candidate's campaign site, June 16, 2025

  • ICESaid

    In a campaign TV ad released 2026-03-03, Barr said he supports ICE and helped triple the number of ICE agents; the campaign said the ad backs ICE's deportation operations.

    "I love ICE. That’s why I helped President Trump triple the number of ICE agents."

    Barr Toasts to ICE Crackdown on Illegals, Slams Open Borders Socialists like Charles Booker, Candidate's campaign site, March 3, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    Speaking at a Trump rally at Northern Kentucky University on 2026-10-17, Barr credited President Trump with the results of the U.S. and Israeli strikes on Iran.

    "Thank you, President Trump, for making America and the world a safer place."

    Trump parachutes to KY to stump for Republicans in safe seats, give GOP nod in ‘tight race’, Kentucky Lantern, October 17, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In a campaign statement on 2025-06-26, Barr said he is working to pass the "America First agenda" alongside President Trump and would be the president's ally in the Senate.

    "When I’m in the Senate, I’ll be President Trump’s biggest ally."

    Barr: I’m the only candidate with a proven record of supporting and delivering for Trump, Candidate's campaign site, June 26, 2025

  • TaxesVoted

    On 2025-07-03 Barr voted Aye on House roll call 190, the motion to concur in the Senate amendment to H.R. 1, the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (tax and spending reconciliation bill), which passed 218-214.

    Roll call: Roll Call 190 | Bill Number: H. R. 1, Office of the Clerk, U.S. House of Representatives, July 3, 2025

  • Trade and tariffsSaid

    On 2026-02-20, after a Supreme Court decision on tariffs, Barr issued a statement backing President Trump's decision to reinstate tariffs.

    "I fully support President Trump’s decision to reinstate tariffs."

    Barr Backs Trump Reinstating Tariffs, Calls for Protecting America First Trade Policies following SCOTUS Decision, Office of U.S. Rep. Andy Barr, February 20, 2026

  • Foreign policySaid

    In a House floor speech released 2026-03-05, Barr opposed a war powers resolution on the U.S. military operation against Iran, saying the president does not need congressional permission for it.

    "Operation Epic Fury is neutralizing that threat, and it has been a tremendous success."

    Barr Defends Trump’s Iran Strike, Slams War Powers Resolution as “Dangerous Gift to Our Enemies”, Office of U.S. Rep. Andy Barr, March 5, 2026

Charles Booker

Democratic 3% on Kalshi

Campaign website

On the record

  • IsraelSaid

    His campaign platform says he would propose ending the annual U.S. aid commitment to Israel and suspending future military spending and arms sales to Israel until a two-state solution is in place.

    "On day one, I will introduce a proposal to terminate our annual commitment to Israel that sends nearly $4 billion in foreign aid, and to suspend future military spending and arms sales until a two-state solution is enacted."

    Charles Booker on Keep America Strong. End Reckless Wars. Honor Those Who Serve. | Kentucky U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • ICESaid

    At a WHAS/Courier Journal Democratic primary forum on 2026-04-07, asked about the shutdown and Homeland Security funding after ICE officers shot and killed two U.S. citizens in Minneapolis, Booker said ICE should be abolished and that there was "no negotiating on this topic."

    "needs to be abolished"

    KY Democratic Senate candidates talk government shutdowns and Trump’s Iran war in forum, Kentucky Lantern, April 7, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    At the same 2026-04-07 forum, held as President Trump threatened Iran over the Strait of Hormuz, Booker said Trump's social media posts had been "essentially threatening genocide" and that this could not be waved away.

    "essentially threatening genocide"

    KY Democratic Senate candidates talk government shutdowns and Trump’s Iran war in forum, Kentucky Lantern, April 7, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In his opening remarks at the 2026-04-07 forum, right after addressing Trump's threats against Iran, Booker said democracy was at stake and described his campaign as offering ideas on poverty and Kentuckians' needs.

    "We are in a moment where democracy is at stake — humanity is at stake — and so this conversation is not just about what we need as Kentuckians. It’s about our very future"

    KY Democratic Senate candidates talk government shutdowns and Trump’s Iran war in forum, Kentucky Lantern, April 7, 2026

  • Trade and tariffsSaid

    His campaign platform says he would introduce a resolution ending the national emergency President Trump has used to impose tariffs, which he says have raised prices.

    "On day one, I will introduce a joint resolution revoking the fake “national emergency” that President Trump has used to raise tariffs that have made everything cost more and hurt Kentucky businesses."

    Charles Booker on Making Life More Affordable | Kentucky U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • Foreign policySaid

    His campaign platform says he would support a War Powers Resolution ending funding for the U.S. war in Iran and bringing troops home.

    "On day one, I will join my colleagues in supporting a War Powers Resolution that will end funding for the war in Iran, bring our troops home, and cut the costs at the pump for Kentuckians."

    Charles Booker on Keep America Strong. End Reckless Wars. Honor Those Who Serve. | Kentucky U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • Health careSaid

    His campaign platform calls for restoring the enhanced Affordable Care Act subsidies, a state-run public insurance option, and expanded Medicare drug price negotiation.

    "Restoring the Affordable Care Act subsidies that Andy Barr voted to eliminate"

    Charles Booker on Making Healthcare Affordable for All Kentuckians | Kentucky U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • AbortionSaid

    His campaign platform cites Kentucky voters' 2022 rejection of a constitutional amendment on abortion and says decisions on pregnancy, contraception and IVF belong to women and their doctors.

    "Choices about pregnancy, contraception, IVF and miscarriage care belong to women, their families and their doctors, not Washington."

    Charles Booker on Making Healthcare Affordable for All Kentuckians | Kentucky U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • Money in politicsSaid

    His campaign platform supports congressional term limits, a ban on members of Congress trading stock, and a ban on members becoming lobbyists after leaving office.

    "Banning members of Congress from trading stock while they're in office."

    Charles Booker on Fighting Corruption & Draining the Swamp in Washington | Kentucky U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

Your script

Hi, my name is ____, and I'm a constituent from ____, ZIP ____.

I'm calling about AIPAC money, ICE and checks and balances.

I want my senator to refuse money from AIPAC PAC and United Democracy Project, and to say so publicly.

I want my senator to vote against more money for ICE detention and enforcement.

I want my senator to use Congress's oversight power, including hearings, subpoenas and votes, whichever party is in charge.

Please record my position. Thank you for your time.

Some offices log one issue per call. If they do, call back for the rest.

Call

    Tips for calling
    • Call your own senators and representative. Offices mostly count calls from their own voters.
    • Say your ZIP so the call counts as a constituent.
    • You'll talk to a staff member, not the official. That's normal: they log your call.
    • One or two issues per call is best.
    • Voicemail counts. Offices go through it every day.
    • If the D.C. office is busy, try a state office, or the U.S. Capitol Switchboard at (202) 224-3121.
    • Call again next week.
