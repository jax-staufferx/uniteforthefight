Texas Senate race

Open seat: John Cornyn holds it and is not on the ballot. Candidates: the Democratic and Republican nominees, incumbent first.

Ken Paxton

Republican 39% on Kalshi

Texas Attorney General · Campaign website

On the record

  • IsraelSaid

    On January 6, 2026, Paxton's office announced Texas had joined a multistate amicus brief backing survivors and families of victims of Hamas's October 7, 2023 attack in an Anti-Terrorism Act suit against American Muslims for Palestine and National Students for Justice in Palestine. In the announcement, Paxton said Hamas and its domestic supporters must be dismantled.

    "Radical Islamic terrorist groups like Hamas must be decimated and dismantled, and that includes their domestic supporting branches"

    Attorney General Ken Paxton Files Legal Brief Against American Muslims for Palestine and Other Radical Groups to Combat Hamas Terrorism, Office of the Texas Attorney General, January 6, 2026

  • ICESaid

    On October 22, 2025, Paxton's office announced that its Criminal Investigative Division had detained 35 people and turned them over to ICE for deportation under a Section 287(g) agreement with DHS and ICE. Paxton credited the partnership with the Trump administration.

    "Through our partnership with the Trump Administration, Texas is a safer place. Every illegal must be found, detained, and deported"

    Attorney General Ken Paxton Arrests 35 Illegal Aliens for Immigration Violations Through Partnership with Trump Administration, Office of the Texas Attorney General, October 22, 2025

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In an April 29, 2025 statement on President Trump's first 100 days back in office, Paxton praised Trump, saying he had secured the borders and restored the rule of law, and said he was proud to stand with the president.

    "In his first 100 days, President Donald Trump has set the new standard for bold, effective leadership."

    Attorney General Ken Paxton Issues Statement on President Donald Trump's First 100 Days in Office, Office of the Texas Attorney General, April 29, 2025

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In his May 26, 2026 victory speech after winning the Republican runoff over Sen. John Cornyn, Paxton called President Trump the leader of the party and said he looks forward to working with him in the Senate.

    "I'm honored to have his support, and I look forward to working with him in the Senate to deliver for Texas."

    Texas Republicans nominate Ken Paxton for Senate seat, NPR Morning Edition via Texas Public Radio, May 26, 2026

  • Social Security and MedicareSaid

    After a leaked recording in which he said cuts to mandatory federal spending were unavoidable, Paxton said in an October 2026 statement to Houston Public Media that he would not vote to cut Social Security benefits.

    "Social Security represents a sacred promise that seniors paid into, and they have earned every single dollar."

    Paxton says he won't cut Social Security benefits, responding to leaked audio, Houston Public Media via Texas Public Radio, October 14, 2026

  • Health careSaid

    At an August 5, 2026 campaign rally in Houston, Paxton said the centerpiece of his economic plan is a tax deduction of $25,000 per taxpayer and dependent for medical expenses, including health insurance premiums.

    "Obviously, medical expenses have gone up, and they've gotten out of control, and so we need to help Texans with those"

    Ken Paxton touts economic plan, bashes Senate race rival James Talarico at Houston rally, Houston Public Media via Texas Public Radio, August 5, 2026

  • HousingSaid

    At the same August 5, 2026 Houston rally, Paxton proposed a $50,000 tax deduction for first- and second-time homebuyers buying a primary residence.

    "I also am offering up a $50,000 tax deduction for first-time homebuyers and second-time homebuyers if they're buying for their primary residence"

    Ken Paxton touts economic plan, bashes Senate race rival James Talarico at Houston rally, Houston Public Media via Texas Public Radio, August 5, 2026

James Talarico

Democratic 62% on Kalshi

Texas State Representative · Campaign website

On the record

  • IsraelSaid

    At the January 24, 2026 Democratic primary debate, Talarico said Israel has a right to defend itself after the October 7 attacks, and said he would use U.S. financial and diplomatic leverage to end the war in Gaza, including banning offensive weapons to the Netanyahu government.

    "I will use every bit of this country's financial and diplomatic leverage to end the death and destruction in Gaza, which means banning offensive weapons to the Netanyahu government"

    Crockett, Talarico take on affordability, ICE and Trump during Texas primary debate, Houston Public Media via Texas Public Radio, January 24, 2026

  • ICESaid

    At an October 14, 2026 CNN town hall in Fort Worth, Talarico said he does not support abolishing ICE, but said that over the past year the agency has acted as a "secret police force."

    "We need Immigration and Customs Enforcement in this country, but that's not what we're seeing. What we've seen over the last year is a secret police force"

    Talarico says he doesn't support abolishing ICE but criticizes their work under Trump as 'secret police', Houston Public Media via Texas Public Radio, October 14, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In a May 29, 2026 NPR interview, Talarico said many 2024 Trump voters expected him to lower costs, drain the swamp, end the forever wars and release the Epstein files, and that the president had failed on all four.

    "a little over a year into this administration, the president has failed on all four counts"

    Texas State Democratic Rep. James Talarico on race to flip Senate seat, NPR Morning Edition via Texas Public Radio, May 29, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    His campaign's health care priorities (campaign website, page undated, viewed October 18, 2026) call for reversing Trump administration cuts to ACA tax credits, Medicare and Medicaid.

    "Protect and improve the Affordable Care Act (ACA) and reverse the Trump Administration's dangerous cuts to the ACA enhanced tax credits, Medicare, and Medicaid"

    James Talarico on Health Care & Prescription Drug Costs, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • Foreign policySaid

    In a March 20, 2026 clip posted by his campaign and shown on his foreign policy page, Talarico said the Iraq War cost the country lives, money and moral standing, and that he worries the administration is repeating that mistake. The same page lists ending the war in Iran as a priority.

    "I worry this administration is making the same mistake with another forever war."

    James Talarico on Foreign Policy, Iran & Israel, Candidate's campaign site, March 20, 2026

  • Money in politicsSaid

    At the October 14, 2026 CNN town hall, Talarico said super PACs of both parties should be banned.

    "I think we got to ban them all because there's way too much influence from big money in our politics"

    At CNN town hall, Senate candidate James Talarico woos moderate and undecided voters, The Texas Tribune via KUT, October 14, 2026

  • The economySaid

    At an August 9, 2026 rally in Pasadena, Texas, Talarico called for raising the minimum wage, rolling back tax breaks for the wealthy in favor of the middle class and small businesses, and strengthening unions.

    "The American dream has become an American nightmare"

    James Talarico leans into populist message in Pasadena, attacking Ken Paxton and 'billionaire megadonors', Houston Public Media via Texas Public Radio, August 9, 2026

  • TechnologySaid

    At an August 5, 2026 rally in Arlington, Talarico called for regulating data centers and proposed an "AI dividend" giving Americans a portion of data center profits.

    "If data center companies can't meet our demands, they shouldn't be built in our communities"

    Data center industry contributes thousands to Paxton campaign, KERA News via Texas Public Radio, August 5, 2026

  • Workers and unionsSaid

    At a July 30, 2026 campaign stop at a plumbers and pipefitters union training center in San Antonio, Talarico said strengthening organized labor is key to rebuilding the middle class.

    "You can almost track the destruction of the middle class with the destruction of organized labor"

    Talarico touts labor, higher wages, opposition to ICE policies during San Antonio campaign stop, Texas Public Radio, July 30, 2026

Your script

Hi, my name is ____, and I'm a constituent from ____, ZIP ____.

I'm calling about AIPAC money, ICE and checks and balances.

I want my senator to refuse money from AIPAC PAC and United Democracy Project, and to say so publicly.

I want my senator to vote against more money for ICE detention and enforcement.

Call

    Tips for calling
    • Call your own senators and representative. Offices mostly count calls from their own voters.
    • Say your ZIP so the call counts as a constituent.
    • You'll talk to a staff member, not the official. That's normal: they log your call.
    • One or two issues per call is best.
    • Voicemail counts. Offices go through it every day.
    • If the D.C. office is busy, try a state office, or the U.S. Capitol Switchboard at (202) 224-3121.
    • Call again next week.
