Colorado Senate race

John Hickenlooper holds the seat and is running again. Candidates: the Democratic and Republican nominees, incumbent first.

John Hickenlooper

Democratic Incumbent99% on Kalshi

U.S. senator, running for re-election · Campaign website

On the record

  • IsraelVoted

    On April 15, 2026, Hickenlooper voted yes on a motion to discharge S.J.Res. 32, a joint resolution disapproving a proposed U.S. foreign military sale of defense articles and services to the Government of Israel. The motion failed 40-59.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate Roll Call Votes 119th Congress - 2nd Session (Vote Number 80), U.S. Senate, April 15, 2026

  • ICESaid

    After voting no on the Department of Homeland Security funding bill on Feb. 12, 2026, Hickenlooper said ICE needs an overhaul and called for body cameras, judicial warrants, and strict limits on the use of force.

    "The American people have seen with their own eyes that ICE is out of control. The agency needs a top-to-bottom overhaul."

    Hickenlooper Statement After NO Vote on DHS Funding Bill, Office of Sen. John Hickenlooper (senate.gov), February 12, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In an Oct. 14, 2026 debate with Republican Mark Baisley, Hickenlooper said he regrets his early-2025 votes to confirm many of Trump's Cabinet nominees, votes he had earlier defended as reducing harm to Colorado.

    "I regret the confirmation of every one of those cabinet members. Every single one"

    Hickenlooper debates Trump, healthcare with GOP challenger Baisley, Colorado Newsline, October 14, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In the same Oct. 14, 2026 debate, Hickenlooper said his opponent supports every action of President Trump and asked voters to send him back to Washington to bring change.

    "We need change in Washington. A vote for Mr. Baisley would be two years more of the same."

    Hickenlooper debates Trump, healthcare with GOP challenger Baisley, Colorado Newsline, October 14, 2026

  • Foreign policySaid

    On Aug. 6, 2026, Hickenlooper introduced a War Powers Resolution directing the President to end U.S. hostilities against Iran. His office said he had voted 13 times to end the war.

    "We need to get out of Iran and get back to lowering costs for working families."

    Hickenlooper Introduces War Powers Resolution to End Iran War, Office of Sen. John Hickenlooper (senate.gov), August 6, 2026

  • The economySaid

    On April 23, 2026, after voting against a Senate budget bill that would authorize an additional $70 billion for immigration enforcement, Hickenlooper said Congress should instead address the cost of living, which he attributed to the Iran war, tariffs, and health care cuts.

    "Americans are asking their government for relief from the cost-of-living emergency driven by this president’s unwanted war, tariffs, and health care cuts"

    Hickenlooper Votes Against Republicans' ICE Funding Bill that Does Nothing to Lower Prices, Office of Sen. John Hickenlooper (senate.gov), April 23, 2026

  • TaxesVoted

    On July 1, 2025, Hickenlooper voted no on Senate passage of H.R. 1, the budget reconciliation bill known as the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, which extended the 2017 tax cuts. The vote was 50-50, and the Vice President voted yea to pass it.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate Roll Call Votes 119th Congress - 1st Session (Vote Number 372), U.S. Senate, July 1, 2025

On the record

  • Israel

    No public statement found as of October 18, 2026.

  • ICESaid

    In an Aspen Times candidate questionnaire published Oct. 7, 2026, asked about increased federal immigration enforcement in Colorado, Baisley said the increase was a response to Biden administration policies, and said he would encourage Homeland Security to use only nonlethal force against people who interfere with enforcement.

    "Of course, the increased enforcement is the necessary response to the irresponsible policies of the Biden Administration"

    Q&A: Meet the candidates running to represent Colorado in the US Senate, The Aspen Times, October 7, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    At the 9NEWS Senate debate on Oct. 14, 2026, asked about President Trump's deal to buy Russian diesel fuel, Baisley said he wished the U.S. did not have to go to Russia but would trust the president's decision.

    "I'm going to trust that the president is making a wise decision in the long run. But it's really painful to see."

    Hickenlooper, Baisley clash on affordability, health care and Iran war in 9NEWS Senate debate, 9NEWS (via Yahoo News), October 14, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    At the Oct. 14, 2026 debate, after Hickenlooper said Baisley "supports every action of President Trump" and would be a "rubber stamp," Baisley said his model was Thomas Jefferson's call for a "wise and frugal government."

    "If I'm anybody's sycophant, it's Thomas Jefferson's"

    Hickenlooper debates Trump, healthcare with GOP challenger Baisley, Colorado Newsline, October 14, 2026

  • Foreign policySaid

    At the Oct. 14, 2026 debate, asked about the U.S. war with Iran, Baisley said it responds to decades of conflict with Iran and backed the president's action; he said he did not support U.S. ground troops in Iran.

    "It's about time that a president stand up to them and put them back where they ought to be, and back in their country, and not harming others"

    Hickenlooper, Baisley clash on affordability, health care and Iran war in 9NEWS Senate debate, 9NEWS (via Yahoo News), October 14, 2026

  • AbortionSaid

    At the Oct. 14, 2026 debate, Baisley restated his opposition to abortion rights and said he would vote for a national abortion ban.

    "slavery decision of our generation"

    Hickenlooper debates Trump, healthcare with GOP challenger Baisley, Colorado Newsline, October 14, 2026

  • Health careSaid

    At the Oct. 14, 2026 debate, Baisley said he would have voted for the 2025 Republican tax and spending law and suggested government help people pay for private insurance rather than government-subsidized options like Medicaid.

    "Health care is just no exception to the free market forces"

    Hickenlooper, Baisley clash on affordability, health care and Iran war in 9NEWS Senate debate, 9NEWS (via Yahoo News), October 14, 2026

  • GunsSaid

    In a May 12, 2026 campaign statement, Baisley praised the Justice Department's lawsuit against Colorado's new magazine ban and pledged to support federal concealed-carry reciprocity.

    "Threats do not end at state lines and neither should your right to carry."

    5-12-26 Senator Mark Baisley Defends Second Amendment Rights Following DOJ Lawsuit, Candidate's campaign site, May 12, 2026

  • FarmingSaid

    On Sept. 14, 2026, Baisley released a "Farm Prosperity and Cost Relief Act" proposal of tax deductions for farm inputs, rules for deducting equipment leases, and voluntary conservation incentives.

    "America cannot have food security without farm security"

    Press Release — Senator Mark Baisley Releases Farm Prosperity Proposal to Lower the Cost of Doing Business for America's Farmers and Ranchers, Candidate's campaign site, September 14, 2026

Your script

Hi, my name is ____, and I'm a constituent from ____, ZIP ____.

I'm calling about AIPAC money, ICE and checks and balances.

I want my senator to refuse money from AIPAC PAC and United Democracy Project, and to say so publicly.

I want my senator to vote against more money for ICE detention and enforcement.

I want my senator to use Congress's oversight power, including hearings, subpoenas and votes, whichever party is in charge.

Please record my position. Thank you for your time.

Some offices log one issue per call. If they do, call back for the rest.

Call

    Tips for calling
    • Call your own senators and representative. Offices mostly count calls from their own voters.
    • Say your ZIP so the call counts as a constituent.
    • You'll talk to a staff member, not the official. That's normal: they log your call.
    • One or two issues per call is best.
    • Voicemail counts. Offices go through it every day.
    • If the D.C. office is busy, try a state office, or the U.S. Capitol Switchboard at (202) 224-3121.
    • Call again next week.
