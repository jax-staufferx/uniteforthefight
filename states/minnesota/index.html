Peggy Flanagan

Democratic 86% on Kalshi

Lieutenant Governor of Minnesota · Campaign website

On the record

  • IsraelSaid

    At the Oct. 16, 2026 MPR News debate, Flanagan called the Oct. 7, 2023 Hamas attack horrific and said the Netanyahu government's response has been deeply destructive; MPR reported she said she would oppose additional U.S. military aid to Israel under Netanyahu.

    "I also know that the response from Benjamin Netanyahu's government since that time has been deeply destructive."

    Peggy Flanagan, Michele Tafoya square off in testy Minnesota Senate debate, MPR News, October 16, 2026

  • ICESaid

    At the Oct. 16, 2026 debate, asked about last winter's federal immigration enforcement surge in Minnesota, Flanagan said ICE should be replaced.

    "ICE should absolutely be ripped apart, and it should be replaced by a system that absolutely has secure borders, that is grounded in public safety for everyone, dignity, due process, humanity, and a pathway to citizenship for folks who have been here who are law-abiding"

    Peggy Flanagan, Michele Tafoya square off in testy Minnesota Senate debate, MPR News, October 16, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    Her campaign's Priorities page (last updated March 27, 2026, per the page's metadata) says President Trump is abusing his office.

    "Donald Trump is the worst offender, abusing the most powerful office in the world to line his own pockets, unlawfully target and punish his personal enemies, and destroy programs that everyday Americans rely on."

    Priorities - Peggy Flanagan for Minnesota, Candidate's campaign site, March 27, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    Her campaign's Priorities page (last updated March 27, 2026) says Trump threatens democracy and that Democrats must do more than make modest improvements.

    "Trump is seriously threatening our democracy, and Democrats' focus cannot be to simply make life only a little less lousy for folks. We must recognize that this is not normal and stand together."

    Priorities - Peggy Flanagan for Minnesota, Candidate's campaign site, March 27, 2026

  • Trade and tariffsSaid

    At the Oct. 16, 2026 debate, Flanagan said current tariffs are hurting Minnesota farmers and that Congress should assert its authority over tariffs.

    "I think Congress has to do their job, and we need to send people who are going to stand up to this president and realize that you know tariffs are under the control of Congress."

    Peggy Flanagan, Michele Tafoya square off in testy Minnesota Senate debate, MPR News, October 16, 2026

  • Social Security and MedicareSaid

    At the Oct. 16, 2026 debate, Flanagan said she would avoid Social Security benefit cuts by applying the payroll tax to higher earnings.

    "We have to remove the cap for $250,000 or higher"

    Peggy Flanagan, Michele Tafoya square off in testy Minnesota Senate debate, MPR News, October 16, 2026

  • Foreign policySaid

    At the Oct. 16, 2026 debate, Flanagan said she opposed the war with Iran; MPR reported she committed to voting to end it.

    "This war is costing us financially, but also in lives."

    Peggy Flanagan, Michele Tafoya square off in testy Minnesota Senate debate, MPR News, October 16, 2026

  • Health careSaid

    Her campaign's Priorities page (last updated March 27, 2026) says she supports Medicare for All.

    "Peggy believes healthcare is a human right and will fight for Medicare for All"

    Priorities - Peggy Flanagan for Minnesota, Candidate's campaign site, March 27, 2026

  • Money in politicsSaid

    Her campaign's Priorities page (last updated March 27, 2026) says she does not take corporate PAC money and would push to ban corporate money in elections.

    "In the Senate, Peggy will fight to ban corporate money in our elections and require disclosure of all donors"

    Priorities - Peggy Flanagan for Minnesota, Candidate's campaign site, March 27, 2026

On the record

  • IsraelSaid

    At the Oct. 16, 2026 MPR News debate, Tafoya said she stands with Israel and disputed calling the war in Gaza a genocide.

    "It is not a genocide when you offer medicines to those people and send in life-saving water and food and nutrition to those people, when you drop leaflets to let them know what is coming, that is not a genocide"

    Peggy Flanagan, Michele Tafoya square off in testy Minnesota Senate debate, MPR News, October 16, 2026

  • ICESaid

    On Oct. 13, 2026, presenting a public safety plan, Tafoya criticized how federal agents handled last winter's immigration enforcement surge in Minnesota, while calling for more support for local officers.

    "Our local officers should not be big-footed by the feds like we saw with ICE during Kristi Noem's Metro Surge disaster, but we can get them more support."

    Michele Tafoya shrugs off Trump endorsement in close Senate race in Minnesota, Minnesota Reformer, October 13, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    On Oct. 13, 2026, shortly after President Trump endorsed her, Tafoya said Trump's recent call for Minnesota to "fight back" against Somali immigrants was "not appropriate."

    "not appropriate"

    Michele Tafoya shrugs off Trump endorsement in close Senate race in Minnesota, Minnesota Reformer, October 13, 2026

  • Where they want the country to go

    No public statement found as of October 18, 2026.

  • Foreign policySaid

    At the Oct. 16, 2026 debate, Tafoya, who earlier in the year supported U.S. intervention in Iran, said the war should end quickly but strategically.

    "The prices are too high. This war does need to end, and it needs to end quickly"

    Peggy Flanagan, Michele Tafoya square off in testy Minnesota Senate debate, MPR News, October 16, 2026

  • Trade and tariffsSaid

    At the Oct. 16, 2026 debate, Tafoya said tariffs are a tool to bring other countries to the table to renegotiate trade agreements.

    "What we're asking for is to level the playing field, to bring partners to the table to say, let's renegotiate this."

    Peggy Flanagan, Michele Tafoya square off in testy Minnesota Senate debate, MPR News, October 16, 2026

  • AbortionSaid

    At the Oct. 16, 2026 debate, Tafoya called herself a pro-choice Republican and said she would support reasonable access to abortion in the first three months of pregnancy.

    "I have no desire from the United States Senate to change that law. I have taken a lot of heat from my party for being a pro-choice Republican"

    Peggy Flanagan, Michele Tafoya square off in testy Minnesota Senate debate, MPR News, October 16, 2026

  • ImmigrationSaid

    Her campaign's Issues page (last updated Feb. 18, 2026, per the page's metadata) says immigrants convicted of fraud should be deported and that she would cosponsor the Deporting Fraudsters Act.

    "Any immigrant who is a guest in our country and gets convicted of fraud must be deported."

    Issues - Michele Tafoya for U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, February 18, 2026

Your script

Hi, my name is ____, and I'm a constituent from ____, ZIP ____.

I'm calling about AIPAC money, ICE and checks and balances.

I want my senator to refuse money from AIPAC PAC and United Democracy Project, and to say so publicly.

I want my senator to vote against more money for ICE detention and enforcement.

I want my senator to use Congress's oversight power, including hearings, subpoenas and votes, whichever party is in charge.

Please record my position. Thank you for your time.

Some offices log one issue per call. If they do, call back for the rest.

Call

    Tips for calling
    • Call your own senators and representative. Offices mostly count calls from their own voters.
    • Say your ZIP so the call counts as a constituent.
    • You'll talk to a staff member, not the official. That's normal: they log your call.
    • One or two issues per call is best.
    • Voicemail counts. Offices go through it every day.
    • If the D.C. office is busy, try a state office, or the U.S. Capitol Switchboard at (202) 224-3121.
    • Call again next week.
