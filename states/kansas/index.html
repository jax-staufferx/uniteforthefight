Kansas Senate race

Roger Marshall holds the seat and is running again. Candidates: the Democratic and Republican nominees, incumbent first.

Roger Marshall

Republican Incumbent65% on Kalshi

U.S. senator, running for re-election · Campaign website

On the record

  • IsraelSaid

    On Fox Radio's Brian Kilmeade Show on June 22, 2026, discussing the memorandum of understanding with Iran and the U.S. relationship with Israel, Marshall said he has long supported Israel and described himself as both pro-Israel and America First.

    "Yeah, certainly I’m pro-Israel, but I’m absolutely America First."

    Senator Marshall: No Forever Wars, No Nukes, & The Strait Stays Open, Office of U.S. Sen. Roger Marshall (senate.gov), June 22, 2026

  • ICESaid

    In a Sept. 24, 2026 statement on ICE activity in southwest Kansas, Marshall said ICE and Kansas law enforcement had worked well together, but that in recent days local officers were not given advance notice or cooperation by ICE, causing mass disruptions to communities and businesses. He said he relayed the concerns to the Department of Homeland Security.

    "We must keep our communities safe, but it has to be done in a responsible and secure manner."

    Senator Marshall Issues Statement on ICE in Southwest Kansas, Office of U.S. Sen. Roger Marshall (senate.gov), September 24, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In a Feb. 25, 2026 statement on President Trump's State of the Union address, Marshall credited Trump with tax relief, border security and lower inflation and violent crime.

    "the President is delivering real results for hardworking families in Kansas and across the nation."

    Senator Marshall Praises President Trump’s State of the Union Address, Office of U.S. Sen. Roger Marshall (senate.gov), February 25, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In the same Feb. 25, 2026 statement, Marshall said the President had made clear that families are in a better financial position but the work is not done, and that he would keep working with Trump.

    "We will continue to work with President Trump to make the American Dream more easily attainable for families all across this nation."

    Senator Marshall Praises President Trump’s State of the Union Address, Office of U.S. Sen. Roger Marshall (senate.gov), February 25, 2026

  • Trade and tariffsSaid

    At an Oct. 16, 2026 candidate forum in Topeka, a Kansas business executive told Marshall that President Trump's tariffs had hurt his company. Marshall said most manufacturers he has talked to are expanding and that the tariffs are bringing jobs back to the U.S.

    "I think most manufacturing across the state of Kansas is growing because of these tariffs."

    Roger Marshall praises Trump's tariff policies after Kansas executive says they hurt his business, Kansas Reflector, October 16, 2026

  • Foreign policySaid

    On Fox Radio on June 22, 2026, Marshall said he supported the Trump administration's memorandum of understanding with Iran, naming its goals as no prolonged war, no Iranian nuclear weapon, an open Strait of Hormuz and lower gas and grocery prices.

    "I think at the end of the day, what it does is no forever wars, no nukes, the straits stay open, and it’s going to bring the cost of gas and groceries down."

    Senator Marshall: No Forever Wars, No Nukes, & The Strait Stays Open, Office of U.S. Sen. Roger Marshall (senate.gov), June 22, 2026

  • Democracy and electionsSaid

    On NBC's "Meet the Press" on June 28, 2026, asked about the SAVE America Act's proof-of-citizenship and photo ID requirements for voting, Marshall said Americans do not feel elections are trustworthy and supported the requirements.

    "Why not have voter ID? Why not have some type of proof of citizenship."

    U.S. Sen. Roger Marshall of Kansas promises to refuse Trump administration appointment, Kansas Reflector, June 28, 2026

  • The economySaid

    At the Sept. 12, 2026 Kansas State Fair debate, Marshall said he disagreed with President Trump's promise to pay $5,000 to every U.S. adult if Republicans won the midterms, and said he would focus on Kansas jobs, agriculture and the cattle industry.

    "This is not the first time I’ve disagreed with President Trump"

    Clamorous crowd dominates first Kansas U.S. Senate debate between Marshall, Hamilton, Kansas Reflector, September 12, 2026

Adam Hamilton

Democratic 37% on Kalshi

Campaign website

On the record

  • IsraelSaid

    In a Politico video interview reported on 2026-10-02, Hamilton said he wants to end U.S. offensive weapons aid to Israel while keeping defensive weapons, called the war in Gaza "irresponsible and unjust," and said he would not use the word "genocide" to describe it.

    "I don’t want my tax money being used for weapons that are going to be dropped on hospitals, destroying entire communities"

    Kansas Democratic Senate candidate wants to cut offensive aid to Israel, rejects genocide label, Jewish Insider, October 2, 2026

  • ICESaid

    On 2026-09-23, responding to ICE enforcement operations in Dodge City, Garden City and Liberal, Hamilton issued a statement saying local leaders had not been told what was happening and that federal enforcement should focus on serious criminals in coordination with local officials.

    "Federal enforcement should focus on serious criminals in coordination with local officials and Kansas families shouldn’t live in fear."

    U.S. senators respond to increased ICE enforcement in southwest Kansas, KCTV5, September 23, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In a July 2026 interview with United Methodist News, Hamilton said what the Trump administration had been doing since January 2025 was part of why he entered the race, citing SNAP benefit cuts for Kansas children.

    "Starting in January 2025, I was watching things that the Trump administration was doing that were an assault on American values and an assault on Christian values"

    On the road with Rev. Adam Hamilton in Kansas, United Methodist News, July 17, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    His campaign site says he is running because Washington is "making life harder for Kansans," naming tariffs, foreign wars and leadership in Washington as causes of higher costs.

    "Tariffs, foreign wars, and erratic leadership have made the basic necessities like food, medicine, and gas more expensive."

    Adam Hamilton for Kansas, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • Trade and tariffsSaid

    His campaign issues page says he would require congressional approval for new tariffs and publication of their cost to families before they take effect.

    "Adam would prevent future administrations from harming Kansans by requiring congressional approval for new tariffs, and to publish the real cost to American families before those tariffs take effect."

    Issues | Adam Hamilton for Kansas, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • Health careSaid

    His campaign issues page calls for reversing the federal Medicaid cuts and restoring the enhanced ACA premium tax credits.

    "Reverse the cuts to Medicaid and restore the ACA subsidies that hold premiums down."

    Issues | Adam Hamilton for Kansas, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • AbortionSaid

    His campaign issues page supports abortion rights and writing access to contraception into federal law.

    "Protect a woman’s right to choose and prevent further government interference into personal decisions by codifying the access to contraception into law."

    Issues | Adam Hamilton for Kansas, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • ImmigrationSaid

    At the 2026-09-12 Kansas State Fair debate, Hamilton called immigration under President Biden a "disaster" and said the approach since then has also been one; he supports a year-round H-2A farmworker visa program.

    "What we’ve done since then is a disaster, too"

    Kansas U.S. Senate candidates tangle over Trump, agriculture and immigration in State Fair debate, KCUR / Kansas News Service, September 12, 2026

  • Money in politicsSaid

    His campaign issues page calls for banning members of Congress and their households from trading individual stocks while in office.

    "Prohibit members of Congress and their households from buying or selling individual stocks while in office."

    Issues | Adam Hamilton for Kansas, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

Your script

Hi, my name is ____, and I'm a constituent from ____, ZIP ____.

I'm calling about AIPAC money, ICE and checks and balances.

I want my senator to refuse money from AIPAC PAC and United Democracy Project, and to say so publicly.

I want my senator to vote against more money for ICE detention and enforcement.

I want my senator to use Congress's oversight power, including hearings, subpoenas and votes, whichever party is in charge.

Please record my position. Thank you for your time.

Some offices log one issue per call. If they do, call back for the rest.

Call

    Tips for calling
    • Call your own senators and representative. Offices mostly count calls from their own voters.
    • Say your ZIP so the call counts as a constituent.
    • You'll talk to a staff member, not the official. That's normal: they log your call.
    • One or two issues per call is best.
    • Voicemail counts. Offices go through it every day.
    • If the D.C. office is busy, try a state office, or the U.S. Capitol Switchboard at (202) 224-3121.
    • Call again next week.
