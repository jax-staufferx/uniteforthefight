On the record

  • IsraelVoted

    On 2025-07-30 Markey voted Yea on Senate roll call 454, a motion to discharge S.J.Res. 41, a joint resolution of disapproval of a proposed export of certain defense articles to Israel; the motion was rejected 27-70.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate: U.S. Senate Roll Call Votes 119th Congress - 1st Session, U.S. Senate, July 30, 2025

  • ICESaid

    Introducing the "ICE Out of Our Faces Act" in early February 2026, a bill to bar ICE and CBP from using facial recognition and other biometric surveillance, Markey said immigration enforcement under President Trump had become "more aggressive, more lawless."

    "This is a dangerous moment for America under Donald Trump’s immigration enforcement"

    Athol Daily News: Markey introduces ICE Out of Our Faces Act, Candidate's campaign site (reprinting Athol Daily News), February 8, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    At a campaign rally in Worcester on 2026-08-08, Markey said he had voted against more of President Trump's nominees than any other senator.

    "I have voted against more of Donald Trump’s nominees than any other senator because I will never stop standing up to Trump and his dangerous agenda."

    Senator Markey Rallies Worcester Voters, Vows to Keep Delivering for Working Families and Fighting Trump, Candidate's campaign site, August 8, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In his 2026-10-13 WBZ-TV debate with John Deaton, Markey said he would go to Washington to act as a check on President Trump and said Deaton would be a vote for Trump.

    "I’m going to Washington to be a check on Donald Trump. Mr. Deaton is going to Washington to be a blank check for Donald Trump."

    Ed Markey, John Deaton clash in Massachusetts U.S. Senate debate - masslive.com, MassLive, October 13, 2026

  • TaxesVoted

    On 2025-07-01 Markey voted Nay on Senate roll call 372, passage of H.R. 1, the reconciliation bill (One Big Beautiful Bill Act); the Senate split 50-50 and the Vice President voted Yea to pass it.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate: U.S. Senate Roll Call Votes 119th Congress - 1st Session, U.S. Senate, July 1, 2025

  • Foreign policyVoted

    On 2026-06-23 Markey voted Yea on Senate roll call 184, H.Con.Res. 86, directing the President under the War Powers Resolution to remove U.S. armed forces from hostilities with Iran; it was agreed to 50-48.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate: U.S. Senate Roll Call Votes 119th Congress - 2nd Session, U.S. Senate, June 23, 2026

  • Trade and tariffsSaid

    At a Cambridge toy store on 2025-05-16, Markey said he would seek legislation exempting small businesses from President Trump's tariffs.

    "Right now, Trump‘s tariffs are turning ‘Main Street’ into ‘Pain Street’ for small businesses"

    Boston Globe: At Cambridge toy store, Markey blasts Trump for ‘toying with our economy’ with tariffs, Candidate's campaign site (reprinting The Boston Globe), May 16, 2025

On the record

  • IsraelSaid

    In a statement on the third anniversary of the Oct. 7, 2023 Hamas attack on Israel, issued 2026-10-07, Deaton honored the victims and the hostages.

    "Time stands still for the families of victims whose lives were cut short by Hamas terrorists hellbent on inflicting their evil and oppressive ideology on the world."

    John Deaton Statement on 3rd Anniversary of Oct. 7 Attacks by Hamas, Candidate's campaign site, October 7, 2026

  • ICESaid

    His campaign issues page says he supports border security, ending catch-and-release and asylum reform, and opposes both blanket amnesty and mass deportation.

    "I reject both blanket amnesty and mass deportation."

    Issues | John Deaton for U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In a 2026-09-05 campaign statement on the U.S. military operation against Iran, Deaton said the engagement had no congressional approval and called on the Senate to pass a War Powers resolution or set judicial limits on it.

    "That means supporting passage of a War Powers resolution or placing judicial limits on this unconstitutional military action."

    Deaton: Markey Should Back Common-Sense Nuclear Policy as Healey Hosts New England Summit in Lowell, Candidate's campaign site, September 5, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In a July 2026 MassLive candidate questionnaire, Deaton said he is "not beholden to a person or a party" and would judge each bill by whether it helps Massachusetts.

    "My one test is simple: If something is good for Massachusetts, I will support it. If it’s not, I won’t."

    Questionnaire from Republican U.S. Senate candidate John Deaton - masslive.com, MassLive, July 8, 2026

  • ImmigrationSaid

    His campaign issues page supports an earned path to legal status and citizenship for DACA recipients and similar Dreamers who meet strict standards.

    "I support a targeted, earned pathway to legal status and ultimately citizenship for DACA recipients and similarly situated Dreamers who meet rigorous standards"

    Issues | John Deaton for U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • Money in politicsSaid

    His proposed "Clean Hands Act" includes congressional term limits, a lifetime lobbying ban for former members, and a ban on members trading stocks, crypto or commodities.

    "No-Trade Zone — Ban stock, crypto or commodities trading by Members of Congress."

    Issues | John Deaton for U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • Foreign policySaid

    His campaign issues page says U.S. foreign aid and military support must serve U.S. interests and that he would not support aid to any country unless convinced it serves America and Massachusetts.

    "I will not vote for another single dollar to go to any foreign country unless I am convinced it is in the interest of America and the people of Massachusetts."

    Issues | John Deaton for U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • Health careSaid

    "not just to abolish ICE, but actually to completely transform our immigration system to one where immigrants are treated with full and equal rights and have a pathway to citizenship"

    "I support programs like a universal single-payer health plan, where everybody has the right to see a doctor, the right to go to a hospital to not be crushed by medical debt"

    Socialist U.S. Senate candidate in Mass. says he's running to confront crisis of capitalism, WAMC Northeast Public Radio, September 3, 2026

    In the Sept. 3, 2026 WAMC interview, Tache said his program calls for cutting the U.S. military budget by 90%.

    "So, a big part of our program is cutting the military budget by 90%, which may sound jarring to some people to hear."

    Socialist U.S. Senate candidate in Mass. says he's running to confront crisis of capitalism, WAMC Northeast Public Radio, September 3, 2026

I want my senator to refuse money from AIPAC PAC and United Democracy Project, and to say so publicly.

Call

    Tips for calling
    • Call your own senators and representative. Offices mostly count calls from their own voters.
    • Say your ZIP so the call counts as a constituent.
    • You'll talk to a staff member, not the official. That's normal: they log your call.
    • One or two issues per call is best.
    • Voicemail counts. Offices go through it every day.
    • If the D.C. office is busy, try a state office, or the U.S. Capitol Switchboard at (202) 224-3121.
    • Call again next week.
