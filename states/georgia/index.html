Georgia Senate race

Jon Ossoff holds the seat and is running again. Candidates: the Democratic and Republican nominees, incumbent first.

Jon Ossoff

Democratic Incumbent95% on Kalshi

U.S. senator, running for re-election · Campaign website

On the record

  • IsraelVoted

    On April 15, 2026, Ossoff voted yes on a motion to discharge S.J.Res. 32, a joint resolution of disapproval of a proposed foreign military sale of defense articles and services to the Government of Israel. The motion failed 40-59.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate: U.S. Senate Roll Call Votes 119th Congress - 2nd Session (Vote 80), U.S. Senate, April 15, 2026

  • ICESaid

    On Jan. 27, 2026, Ossoff released a report from his investigation that counted more than 1,000 credible reports of human rights abuses in U.S. immigration detention during the first year of the Trump administration. He said Americans want both secure borders and humane treatment of detainees.

    "these reports of unacceptable abuse and mistreatment shock the conscience and demand accountability"

    NEW: Sen. Ossoff Investigation Uncovers Over 1,000 Credible Reports of Human Rights Abuses in Immigration Detention, Office of Sen. Jon Ossoff (U.S. Senate), January 27, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    Before the Oct. 14, 2026 Atlanta Press Club debate, Ossoff said he would ask his opponent, Rep. Mike Collins, why he voted for the tariffs, "the war" and the health care cuts, which Ossoff said have driven prices to record highs.

    "Why he voted for the tariffs and the war and the health care cuts that have driven prices to record highs."

    On Offense At The Atlanta Press Club Debate, Ossoff Puts Collins On Trial For His Abysmal Record And Scandals, Candidate's campaign site, October 14, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In a June 19, 2026 statement after the Department of Homeland Security dropped plans for an ICE detention center in Social Circle, Georgia, Ossoff said he would keep opposing what he called overreach by the Trump administration.

    "I will continue fighting alongside Georgia communities against overreach by this Administration."

    Sen. Ossoff Statement on ICE Detention Facility in Social Circle, Office of Sen. Jon Ossoff (U.S. Senate), June 19, 2026

  • Foreign policySaid

    On Feb. 28, 2026, after President Trump said the U.S. was at war for regime change in Iran, Ossoff said he opposed the war and called on Congress to pass a War Powers Resolution.

    "I oppose yet another regime change war-of-choice in the Middle East."

    Sen. Ossoff Statement on Iran, Office of Sen. Jon Ossoff (U.S. Senate), February 28, 2026

  • Trade and tariffsVoted

    On Oct. 30, 2025, Ossoff voted yes on S.J.Res. 88, a joint resolution terminating the national emergency declared to impose global tariffs. It passed 51-47.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate: U.S. Senate Roll Call Votes 119th Congress - 1st Session (Vote 600), U.S. Senate, October 30, 2025

  • Health careVoted

    On July 1, 2025, Ossoff voted no on passage of H.R. 1, the 2025 budget reconciliation bill. The vote was 50-50 and the Vice President voted yes to pass it.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate: U.S. Senate Roll Call Votes 119th Congress - 1st Session (Vote 372), U.S. Senate, July 1, 2025

  • Money in politicsSaid

    Ossoff's campaign biography (as of October 2026) says his campaigns refuse contributions from corporate PACs and federal lobbyists, and that he has introduced legislation to ban corporate PACs and backed a ban on stock trading by members of Congress.

    "Jon is one of few United States Senators whose campaigns refuse to accept campaign contributions from corporate PACs or federal lobbyists."

    About Jon - Jon Ossoff for U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

Mike Collins

Republican 5% on Kalshi

U.S. Representative for Georgia's 10th district · Campaign website

On the record

  • Israel

    No public statement found as of October 18, 2026.

  • ICESaid

    When President Trump signed Collins's Laken Riley Act into law on Jan. 29, 2025, Collins praised it. The law requires Immigration and Customs Enforcement to detain people in the country illegally who are charged with theft, burglary, assault on police and certain other crimes.

    "We're committed to delivering commonsense solutions to fix our broken immigration system, and the Laken Riley Act delivers it."

    President Trump Signs the Laken Riley Act into Law | Representative Collins, Office of Rep. Mike Collins (U.S. House), January 29, 2025

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In a Feb. 23, 2026 statement naming his guest for the State of the Union address, Collins credited President Trump's first year with gains for law enforcement and lower crime.

    "This past year under President Donald Trump, Americans have seen respect for law enforcement flourish, and crime rates plummet to 100-year lows."

    Representative Mike Collins Announces Sheriff Frank Reynolds as State of the Union Guest | Representative Collins, Office of Rep. Mike Collins (U.S. House), February 23, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    Collins's campaign homepage (as of October 2026) describes him as backing President Trump by passing "America First" legislation in Congress.

    "Whether I'm keeping trucks on the road and supporting my community, or backing President Trump by passing America First legislation in Congress, I'm in the fight."

    Mike Collins for Senate – Join the ride!, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • Foreign policyVoted

    On June 3, 2026, Collins voted no on H.Con.Res. 86, a concurrent resolution directing the President under the War Powers Resolution to remove U.S. armed forces from hostilities with Iran. It passed the House 215-208.

    Roll call: Office of the Clerk, U.S. House of Representatives - Roll Call 199, Office of the Clerk, U.S. House of Representatives, June 3, 2026

  • ImmigrationVoted

    On April 16, 2026, Collins voted no on H.R. 1689, a bill to require the Secretary of Homeland Security to designate Haiti for temporary protected status. It passed the House 224-204.

    Roll call: Office of the Clerk, U.S. House of Representatives - Roll Call 120, Office of the Clerk, U.S. House of Representatives, April 16, 2026

  • TaxesVoted

    On July 3, 2025, Collins voted yes on the House motion to concur in the Senate amendment to H.R. 1, the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. It passed 218-214.

    Roll call: Office of the Clerk, U.S. House of Representatives - Roll Call 190, Office of the Clerk, U.S. House of Representatives, July 3, 2025

  • Democracy and electionsSaid

    On Feb. 11, 2026, Collins welcomed House passage of the SAVE America Act, which he cosponsored, and urged the Senate to pass it.

    "There is no reason why the greatest country on Earth should not have the most secure elections on Earth."

    Rep. Mike Collins Celebrates the House Passage of the SAVE America Act | Representative Collins, Office of Rep. Mike Collins (U.S. House), February 11, 2026

Your script

Hi, my name is ____, and I'm a constituent from ____, ZIP ____.

I'm calling about AIPAC money, ICE and checks and balances.

I want my senator to refuse money from AIPAC PAC and United Democracy Project, and to say so publicly.

I want my senator to vote against more money for ICE detention and enforcement.

I want my senator to use Congress's oversight power, including hearings, subpoenas and votes, whichever party is in charge.

Please record my position. Thank you for your time.

Some offices log one issue per call. If they do, call back for the rest.

Call

    Tips for calling
    • Call your own senators and representative. Offices mostly count calls from their own voters.
    • Say your ZIP so the call counts as a constituent.
    • You'll talk to a staff member, not the official. That's normal: they log your call.
    • One or two issues per call is best.
    • Voicemail counts. Offices go through it every day.
    • If the D.C. office is busy, try a state office, or the U.S. Capitol Switchboard at (202) 224-3121.
    • Call again next week.
