On the record

  • IsraelVoted

    On April 15, 2026, Coons voted no on a motion to discharge S.J.Res. 32, a joint resolution of disapproval of a proposed foreign military sale of defense articles and services to the Government of Israel. The motion failed 40-59.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate: U.S. Senate Roll Call Votes 119th Congress - 2nd Session (Vote 80), U.S. Senate, April 15, 2026

  • ICESaid

    In a June 5, 2026 statement after the Senate passed a reconciliation bill funding Immigration and Customs Enforcement and Customs and Border Protection, Coons said the bill funded immigration enforcement while blocking reforms he called commonsense. He voted against the bill.

    "Senate Republicans have just jammed through another bill to spend taxpayer money on out-of-control and unaccountable immigration enforcement"

    Senator Coons statement on vote on reconciliation bill to fund Trump's mass deportation agenda, Office of Sen. Chris Coons (U.S. Senate), June 5, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In a Senate floor speech on Feb. 10, 2026, Coons said the Trump administration's use of government power against journalists, law firms, universities and critics resembled the conduct of authoritarian governments.

    "the remarks and the actions of our president and vice president, they are chillingly rhyming with those of dictators like President Xi and President Putin of the PRC and Russia"

    EXCERPTS: Senator Coons warns that Trump administration's unchecked use of government power could threaten the foundation of democracy in Senate floor speech, Office of Sen. Chris Coons (U.S. Senate), February 10, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    Coons's campaign priorities page (as of October 2026) says President Trump is making costs and health care access worse, and that the country needs to get "back on track."

    "We need to be clear-eyed about what it will take to get America back on track and give Americans peace of mind about their future."

    Priorities | Chris Coons, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • Foreign policyVoted

    On June 23, 2026, Coons voted yes on H.Con.Res. 86, a concurrent resolution directing the President under the War Powers Resolution to remove U.S. armed forces from hostilities with Iran. It was agreed to 50-48.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate: U.S. Senate Roll Call Votes 119th Congress - 2nd Session (Vote 184), U.S. Senate, June 23, 2026

  • Trade and tariffsVoted

    On Oct. 30, 2025, Coons voted yes on S.J.Res. 88, a joint resolution terminating the national emergency declared to impose global tariffs. It passed 51-47.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate: U.S. Senate Roll Call Votes 119th Congress - 1st Session (Vote 600), U.S. Senate, October 30, 2025

  • Health careVoted

    On July 1, 2025, Coons voted no on passage of H.R. 1, the 2025 budget reconciliation bill. The vote was 50-50 and the Vice President voted yes to pass it. His campaign site says the bill "slashed access to Medicaid."

    Roll call: U.S. Senate: U.S. Senate Roll Call Votes 119th Congress - 1st Session (Vote 372), U.S. Senate, July 1, 2025

Mike Katz

Republican 2% on Kalshi

Campaign website · On the ballot as Michael "Dr. Mike" Katz

On the record

  • IsraelSaid

    Katz's campaign "Fire Coons" page (as of October 2026), criticizing Sen. Chris Coons's fundraising, lists support from AIPAC-linked donors and pro-Israel PACs among the special-interest money Coons accepts, and says Katz takes no PAC or special-interest money.

    "Has received substantial support from AIPAC-linked donors and pro-Israel PACs"

    Fire Coons. Hire Katz. — Dr. Mike Katz for U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • ICESaid

    Katz's campaign platform (as of October 2026) calls for strict enforcement of interior immigration laws, withholding federal funds from sanctuary cities, mandatory workplace verification, and faster immigration court decisions followed by deportation when a claim is invalid.

    "This means strictly enforcing interior immigration laws, withholding federal funding from self-declared sanctuary cities that refuse to cooperate with federal law enforcement, and mandating workplace verification systems to protect American labor and wages."

    Platform — Dr. Mike Katz for U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In the "Ending the Weaponization of Government" section of his campaign platform (as of October 2026), Katz says he stands with President Trump against what he calls a two-tiered system of justice.

    "I stand firmly with President Trump in the fight to dismantle this corrupt, two-tiered system of justice."

    Platform — Dr. Mike Katz for U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    Katz's campaign platform (as of October 2026) says he would work with President Trump on federal spending and regulation, including supporting the president's push to cut federal regulations.

    "I will stand firmly with President Trump to halt wasteful federal spending and demand structural accountability in the federal budget."

    Platform — Dr. Mike Katz for U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • ImmigrationSaid

    Katz's campaign platform (as of October 2026) supports finishing the southern border wall, restoring the "Remain in Mexico" policy, and designating drug cartels as foreign terrorist organizations.

    "I fully support finishing physical wall construction in high-traffic sectors"

    Platform — Dr. Mike Katz for U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • GunsSaid

    Katz's campaign platform (as of October 2026) opposes federal and state "red flag" laws, which he calls a violation of due process.

    "I stand firmly with President Trump in absolute opposition to federal "Red Flag" mandates and any attempts to undermine the Second Amendment."

    Platform — Dr. Mike Katz for U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • Democracy and electionsSaid

    Katz's campaign platform (as of October 2026) supports the SAVE Act, which would require documentary proof of U.S. citizenship to register to vote in federal elections.

    "I strongly back the legislative fight led by House Republicans and President Trump to pass and implement the SAVE Act"

    Platform — Dr. Mike Katz for U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • VeteransSaid

    Katz's campaign platform (as of October 2026) commits to starting work on an advanced-care VA facility for southern Delaware within his first 90 days, and to legislation based on the Major Richard Star Act.

    "As Delaware's next U.S. Senator, I will begin to resolve this within my first ninety days in office."

    Platform — Dr. Mike Katz for U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

Your script

Hi, my name is ____, and I'm a constituent from ____, ZIP ____.

I'm calling about AIPAC money, ICE and checks and balances.

I want my senator to refuse money from AIPAC PAC and United Democracy Project, and to say so publicly.

I want my senator to vote against more money for ICE detention and enforcement.

I want my senator to use Congress's oversight power, including hearings, subpoenas and votes, whichever party is in charge.

Please record my position. Thank you for your time.

Some offices log one issue per call. If they do, call back for the rest.

Call

    Tips for calling
    • Call your own senators and representative. Offices mostly count calls from their own voters.
    • Say your ZIP so the call counts as a constituent.
    • You'll talk to a staff member, not the official. That's normal: they log your call.
    • One or two issues per call is best.
    • Voicemail counts. Offices go through it every day.
    • If the D.C. office is busy, try a state office, or the U.S. Capitol Switchboard at (202) 224-3121.
    • Call again next week.
