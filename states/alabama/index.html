Barry Moore

Republican 97% on Kalshi

U.S. Representative for Alabama's 1st congressional district · Campaign website

On the record

  • IsraelSaid

    In an April 20, 2024 statement after voting for H.R. 8034, the Israel Security Supplemental Appropriations Act ($26.3 billion for Israel's defense, including missile defense and the Iron Dome), Moore said the U.S. must support Israel against Hamas.

    "We must stand with Israel and provide the support and resources necessary to rid the Middle East and the world of Hamas’ terrorist regime."

    Rep. Barry Moore supports funding to protect Israel, Office of Rep. Barry Moore (house.gov), April 20, 2024

  • ICESaid

    On June 12, 2026, Moore introduced the TIMEOUT Act, which would bar federal agencies from occupying or maintaining office space in sanctuary jurisdictions that do not cooperate with federal immigration enforcement and require existing federal offices there to relocate.

    "Communities that actively undermine federal immigration enforcement should not continue receiving the economic benefits associated with hosting federal facilities."

    Rep. Barry Moore introduces legislation to relocate federal facilities from sanctuary cities, Office of Rep. Barry Moore (house.gov), June 12, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In a Sept. 25, 2026 statement about President Trump's planned Oct. 2 rally in Mobile, Moore said the rally would energize conservatives and credited the Trump administration with several accomplishments.

    "Thanks to his administration, we are stopping the indoctrination of our children, standing strong on the world stage, and defeating the Radical Democrats."

    Donald Trump to hold rally in Mobile amid Alabama GOP turnout concerns, Alabama Reflector, September 25, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    Moore's campaign biography (last updated March 6, 2026) says he will work with President Trump in the Senate and that the work he and Trump have started should continue.

    "With the border closed, wages growing, and the Deep State vanquished, Barry and President Trump have made incredible strides – but the work is just beginning."

    About - Barry Moore for Alabama, Candidate's campaign site, March 6, 2026

  • Foreign policyVoted

    On Sept. 15, 2026, Moore voted no on H.Con.Res. 93, a concurrent resolution directing the President under the War Powers Resolution to remove U.S. armed forces from hostilities with Iran. The House adopted it.

    Roll call: Office of the Clerk, U.S. House of Representatives - Roll Call 307 (H.Con.Res. 93), Office of the Clerk, U.S. House of Representatives, September 15, 2026

  • Democracy and electionsSaid

    On Feb. 27, 2026, Moore announced his support for the SAVE America Act, which would require photo ID to vote and in-person proof of citizenship to register, and would require states to remove non-citizens from voter rolls.

    "American elections should only be decided by American citizens"

    Rep. Barry Moore supports SAVE America Act to strengthen election integrity, Office of Rep. Barry Moore (house.gov), February 27, 2026

  • ImmigrationSaid

    In April 2026, Moore and three other House Republicans introduced the Americans First Immigration Act, which would change the legal immigration system to select immigrants on merit rather than through the diversity visa lottery.

    "If you want to come to the United States, you should earn it through merit, not diversity lotteries or loopholes."

    Rep. Barry Moore introduces Americans First Immigration Act to put American workers first and advance President Trump's agenda, Office of Rep. Barry Moore (house.gov), April 30, 2026

  • TaxesSaid

    On May 22, 2025, after voting for the House version of the One Big Beautiful Bill reconciliation bill, Moore said it extends the 2017 tax cuts and funds border security.

    "The passage of President Trump's One Big, Beautiful Bill delivers a victory for hardworking families by extending the Trump tax cuts, fully funding border security, lowering energy costs, and investing in new defense technologies."

    Rep. Barry Moore supports President Trump's One, Big, Beautiful Reconciliation Bill, Office of Rep. Barry Moore (house.gov), May 22, 2025

  • The economySaid

    At a May 26, 2026 Shelby County Republican Party candidate forum, Moore said inflation is one of the biggest threats to Alabama families and that he wants to reduce the federal debt.

    "There’s a cost of what we do, and the number one body in the nation that creates inflation is Washington, D.C."

    A voter guide to the Democratic and Republican U.S. Senate runoffs, Alabama Reflector, May 26, 2026

Everett Wess

Democratic 3% on Kalshi

Campaign website

On the record

  • Israel

    No public statement found as of October 18, 2026.

  • ICE

    No public statement found as of October 18, 2026.

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In an interview reported Sept. 25, 2026, Wess described public uncertainty about the Trump administration. The Alabama Reflector reported that he decided to run after sensing voters were dissatisfied with Trump's performance.

    "People are uncertain about what is going to happen next. There is confusion with the current administration, uncertainty with the current administration."

    Democratic U.S. Senate nominee Everett Wess campaigns against long odds, Alabama Reflector, September 25, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In the same interview, Wess spoke about problems with the 2025 budget reconciliation law signed by Trump and said he ran in order to put Alabama residents ahead of the administration.

    "I thought I could put the Alabama citizens first before the wants and the needs of the administration."

    Democratic U.S. Senate nominee Everett Wess campaigns against long odds, Alabama Reflector, September 25, 2026

  • The economySaid

    Wess said in a September 2026 interview that prices have not come down since Trump took office, especially since the start of the Iran war.

    "Gas prices are high, groceries are higher, prescription drugs are also high."

    Democratic U.S. Senate nominee Everett Wess campaigns against long odds, Alabama Reflector, September 25, 2026

  • Workers and unionsSaid

    In a June 9, 2026 phone interview before the Democratic runoff, Wess said his priorities include raising the federal minimum wage, which has been $7.25 an hour since 2009.

    "I want to stabilize the job market, look at things such as increasing minimum wage, no tax on tips for servers"

    A voter guide to the Democratic and Republican U.S. Senate runoffs, Alabama Reflector, June 9, 2026

  • Democracy and electionsSaid

    In the same June 2026 interview, Wess said he wants federal law to prevent racial and partisan gerrymandering.

    "We want to make sure that the voices of our citizens are heard through their vote. We have gerrymandering going on right now"

    A voter guide to the Democratic and Republican U.S. Senate runoffs, Alabama Reflector, June 9, 2026

Your script

Hi, my name is ____, and I'm a constituent from ____, ZIP ____.

I'm calling about AIPAC money, ICE and checks and balances.

I want my senator to refuse money from AIPAC PAC and United Democracy Project, and to say so publicly.

I want my senator to vote against more money for ICE detention and enforcement.

I want my senator to use Congress's oversight power, including hearings, subpoenas and votes, whichever party is in charge.

Please record my position. Thank you for your time.

Some offices log one issue per call. If they do, call back for the rest.

Call

    Tips for calling
    • Call your own senators and representative. Offices mostly count calls from their own voters.
    • Say your ZIP so the call counts as a constituent.
    • You'll talk to a staff member, not the official. That's normal: they log your call.
    • One or two issues per call is best.
    • Voicemail counts. Offices go through it every day.
    • If the D.C. office is busy, try a state office, or the U.S. Capitol Switchboard at (202) 224-3121.
    • Call again next week.
