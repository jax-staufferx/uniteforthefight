Abdul El-Sayed

Democratic 68% on Kalshi

Campaign website

On the record

  • IsraelSaid

    At the Oct. 8, 2026 debate with Mike Rogers in Grand Rapids, El-Sayed said he does not want U.S. tax dollars used to buy weapons for Israel or other countries.

    "I don't want our tax dollars buying weapons for other countries. I don't know what's so hard about that"

    Rogers, El-Sayed debate gets hostile as candidates clash in pivotal US Senate race, Michigan Public, October 8, 2026

  • ICESaid

    His campaign's issues page ("Money Out of Politics" priorities, undated) calls for abolishing ICE and replacing it with immigration enforcement under a new organizational structure.

    "ICE has become a tool for autocracy. Its culture of aggression, impunity, and disregard for the rule of law cannot be reformed or retrained. It must be abolished."

    Money Out of Politics - Abdul for U.S. Senate, Candidate's campaign site, undated page, read October 18, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In a Sept. 2, 2026 campaign statement after a global bond selloff, El-Sayed said the Iran war and trade war under President Trump were raising costs and that Trump was pressuring the Federal Reserve.

    "Yesterday's bond market selloff is a ringing alarm bell about the future of the economy under Trump's mismanagement."

    Abdul El-Sayed: Bond Market Selloff Is A Ringing Alarm Bell About The Future Of The Economy Under Trump's Mismanagement, Candidate's campaign site, September 2, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In an Oct. 8, 2026 statement responding to President Trump's remarks about his name and faith, El-Sayed said Trump's policies are causing suffering and called for change.

    "He thinks he can use my name and my faith to distract us from the suffering his policies are inflicting on us at the pump, at the grocery store, and at the doctor's office. He thinks we don't know any better. It's time for change."

    "He Knows My Name Is Abdul. He Says It Every Day": Dr. Abdul El-Sayed Responds To Trump's Remarks, Candidate's campaign site, October 8, 2026

  • Health careSaid

    In a WDET MichMash interview published Sept. 4, 2026, El-Sayed described the single government health insurance program he supports, with no copays, premiums or deductibles.

    "What I want is a guaranteed health insurance program for every single person from cradle to grave, that doesn't expire until you expire."

    MichMash: Abdul El-Sayed makes his case for the Michigan US Senate seat, WDET 101.9 FM, September 4, 2026

  • Foreign policySaid

    At the Oct. 8, 2026 debate, El-Sayed said the U.S. war with Iran should end, citing its effect on gas and grocery prices.

    "You're going to pay for it, both at the gas tank and at the grocery store, and so we've got to end that war above and beyond pausing the gas tax."

    Rogers, El-Sayed debate gets hostile as candidates clash in pivotal US Senate race, Michigan Public, October 8, 2026

  • Trade and tariffsSaid

    In late September 2026, El-Sayed criticized President Trump's tariffs on Canada, saying tariffs could be used to protect jobs but "this ain't it."

    "There's a way that tariffs could be used to actually protect jobs. This ain't it. Like all things Donald Trump, it is chaotic, ham-fisted, self-serving"

    Anxiety over trade war with Canada provides backdrop for Michigan U.S. Senate race, WDET 101.9 FM, September 29, 2026

  • TaxesSaid

    In an NBC Meet the Press interview aired Oct. 11, 2026 (transcript posted by his campaign), El-Sayed said he supports a wealth tax on billionaires.

    "Billionaires should pay their fair share back into the system. I believe that means we have to pass a wealth tax."

    "Step Up, Pick Up The Baton, And Run As Hard As I Can" — Abdul El-Sayed Sits Down With NBC's Meet The Press, Candidate's campaign site, October 11, 2026

On the record

  • IsraelSaid

    In an Oct. 7, 2026 statement on the anniversary of the Hamas attack on Israel, Rogers pledged to stand with Michigan's Jewish community and for Israel's right to defend itself.

    "You have my word, I will always stand proudly alongside Michigan's Jewish community, against terrorism, and for Israel's right to defend itself."

    Mike Rogers Statement on the Anniversary of October 7th Attacks, Candidate's campaign site, October 7, 2026

  • ICESaid

    In an op-ed published April 15, 2026 in The Midwesterner (reposted on his campaign site), Rogers praised the 2025 tax-and-spending law's funding for border security.

    "The tax cuts also made historic investments in border security to keep criminal illegal aliens such as murderers, rapists, human traffickers, suspected terrorists, and cartels out."

    OP-ED: The Working Families Tax Cuts Are a Game-Changer For Michigan, Candidate's campaign site (op-ed first published in The Midwesterner), April 15, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In his April 15, 2026 op-ed, Rogers credited President Trump with addressing inflation and affordability and praised the "Working Families Tax Cuts" law.

    "As President Trump fights to clean up Biden and the Democrats' mess on inflation and affordability, the historic Working Families Tax Cuts are providing meaningful relief."

    OP-ED: The Working Families Tax Cuts Are a Game-Changer For Michigan, Candidate's campaign site (op-ed first published in The Midwesterner), April 15, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    Ahead of the first debate, Michigan Public reported Rogers saying he can work with the Trump administration on Michigan's problems, and criticizing El-Sayed for wanting to fight with Trump.

    "My opponent, all he wants to do is fight with Trump"

    Rogers, El-Sayed poised to clash in first debate of US Senate race, Michigan Public, October 7, 2026

  • Trade and tariffsSaid

    In a campaign ad released Oct. 7, 2026, Rogers called for ending the tariff war with Canada.

    "here in Michigan, we know that Canada is not our enemy. We need to end this tariff war — now."

    AD: WE NEED TO END THE TARIFF WAR — NOW, Candidate's campaign site, October 7, 2026

  • Foreign policySaid

    In a campaign ad reported Oct. 7, 2026, Rogers, who initially supported the war with Iran, said it should end quickly.

    "The war with Iran needs to end and end quickly"

    Rogers, El-Sayed poised to clash in first debate of US Senate race, Michigan Public, October 7, 2026

  • TaxesSaid

    In his April 15, 2026 op-ed, Rogers said he would make the 2025 tax cuts permanent if elected.

    "When I get to the United States Senate, we will make these tax cuts permanent and keep putting working Michigan families first."

    OP-ED: The Working Families Tax Cuts Are a Game-Changer For Michigan, Candidate's campaign site (op-ed first published in The Midwesterner), April 15, 2026

  • The economySaid

    At an Oct. 15, 2026 rally in Monroe with Vice President JD Vance, Rogers named reading levels, housing costs and manufacturing jobs as his priorities.

    "We're going to bring back those 200,000 manufacturing jobs that the Democrats chased out of our state and overseas"

    JD Vance, Mike Rogers keep up attacks on Abdul El-Sayed during Monroe rally, Michigan Public, October 15, 2026

Your script

Hi, my name is ____, and I'm a constituent from ____, ZIP ____.

I'm calling about AIPAC money, ICE and checks and balances.

I want my senator to refuse money from AIPAC PAC and United Democracy Project, and to say so publicly.

I want my senator to vote against more money for ICE detention and enforcement.

Some offices log one issue per call. If they do, call back for the rest.

Call

    Tips for calling
    • Call your own senators and representative. Offices mostly count calls from their own voters.
    • Say your ZIP so the call counts as a constituent.
    • You'll talk to a staff member, not the official. That's normal: they log your call.
    • One or two issues per call is best.
    • Voicemail counts. Offices go through it every day.
    • If the D.C. office is busy, try a state office, or the U.S. Capitol Switchboard at (202) 224-3121.
    • Call again next week.
