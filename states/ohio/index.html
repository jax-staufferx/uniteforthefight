On the record

  • IsraelVoted

    Roll call: U.S. Senate: Roll Call Vote 119th Congress - 2nd Session (Vote 80), U.S. Senate, April 15, 2026

  • ICESaid

    On June 5, 2026, after the Senate passed the Secure America Act, a reconciliation bill funding U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement and Customs and Border Protection through 2029, he said he supported the bill and that he has consistently voted to fully fund federal law enforcement.

    "Ohioans expect their government to enforce the rule of law, deport criminals, secure the border and keep their communities safe, and that’s exactly what this bill will do."

    Husted votes to fund federal law enforcement, Office of Sen. Jon Husted, June 5, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In a statement after President Trump's 2026 State of the Union address on February 24, 2026, he praised the administration's record on inflation, energy, border security and crime.

    "He highlighted many of the ways we continue to deliver on our promises to make life safer and more affordable for the American people, and particularly the people of Ohio."

    Husted statement on 2026 State of the Union address, Office of Sen. Jon Husted, February 24, 2026

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    On April 9, 2025, accepting President Trump's endorsement for this race, he said he would put "America First" in the Senate and work with the president.

    "I will work diligently with him and his team to secure our borders, champion innovation, restore prosperity to the heartland, and fight for common sense Ohio values."

    President Donald J. Trump Endorses Jon Husted for Senate, Jon Husted for Senate (campaign site), April 9, 2025

  • TaxesVoted

    On July 1, 2025, voted yes on Senate passage of H.R. 1, the budget reconciliation bill that made the 2017 tax cuts permanent (his office calls it the Working Families Tax Cut Act). The bill passed.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate: Roll Call Vote 119th Congress - 1st Session (Vote 372), U.S. Senate, July 1, 2025

  • Health careVoted

    On December 11, 2025, voted no on a motion to invoke cloture on the motion to proceed to S. 3385, a bill to extend the enhanced Affordable Care Act premium tax credit. The motion was rejected.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate: Roll Call Vote 119th Congress - 1st Session (Vote 644), U.S. Senate, December 11, 2025

  • Trade and tariffsVoted

    On October 30, 2025, voted no on S.J.Res. 88, a joint resolution to end the national emergency the president declared to impose global tariffs. The resolution passed the Senate.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate: Roll Call Vote 119th Congress - 1st Session (Vote 600), U.S. Senate, October 30, 2025

  • Democracy and electionsSaid

    In an op-ed published April 17, 2026, the former Ohio secretary of state said he supports requiring photo ID to vote nationwide and had brought an amendment to that effect to the Senate floor.

    "It is because of this success that I have worked in the U.S. Senate to champion simple photo ID requirements at the polls across the country."

    Senate Republicans want to make it easy to vote and hard to cheat in America, Office of Sen. Jon Husted (op-ed first published in the Washington Reporter), April 17, 2026

On the record

  • IsraelVoted

    As a U.S. senator, on November 20, 2024, he voted no on a motion to discharge S.J.Res. 111, a joint resolution of disapproval of a proposed foreign military sale of defense articles and services to the government of Israel. The motion was rejected, 18 to 79.

    Roll call: U.S. Senate: Roll Call Vote 118th Congress - 2nd Session (Vote 292), U.S. Senate, November 20, 2024

  • ICESaid

    In a January 24, 2026 post on his campaign's Bluesky account, he criticized an ICE enforcement action involving a five-year-old boy and said the targeting of innocent families and American citizens is unacceptable.

    "It's unconscionable and immoral that ICE would use a five-year-old boy as bait."

    @sherrodbrownoh.bsky.social on Bluesky, Sherrod Brown (official Bluesky account), January 24, 2026

  • The Trump administrationSaid

    In the video announcing his Senate campaign on August 18, 2025, he said he did not like what he had seen from President Trump and the Republican majority in Congress over the previous eight months, citing tariffs, prices and health coverage.

    "All they’ve done is made things worse for Ohioans, handing over your hard-earned money to corporations and billionaires."

    Sherrod Brown is running in 2026 to return to the U.S. Senate, The Statehouse News Bureau (Ohio public media), August 18, 2025

  • Where they want the country to goSaid

    In a September 24, 2025 post on his campaign's Bluesky account, he said Washington is putting billionaires before workers, pointed to prices, inflation and unemployment, and called for a change of course.

    "Washington is putting billionaires before workers and it’s hurting Ohio families. Prices are up, inflation is up, unemployment is up. It’s time to change course."

    @sherrodbrownoh.bsky.social on Bluesky, Sherrod Brown (official Bluesky account), September 24, 2025

  • Foreign policySaid

    On March 27, 2026, during the U.S. war with Iran, he said he opposes deploying U.S. ground troops to Iran.

    "I strongly oppose putting more American lives in danger by deploying any ground troops to Iran."

    @sherrodbrownoh.bsky.social on Bluesky, Sherrod Brown (official Bluesky account), March 27, 2026

  • Health careSaid

    On July 14, 2026, he called for a law barring drug companies from charging Americans more for a prescription than they charge in other countries.

    "It’s time to stop drug companies from ripping Ohioans off and make it illegal for them to charge Americans more than they do in other countries."

    @sherrodbrownoh.bsky.social on Bluesky, Sherrod Brown (official Bluesky account), July 14, 2026

  • Money in politicsSaid

    On August 21, 2026, he said members of Congress should not trade stocks while in office.

    "If you can’t agree to stop trading stocks, you have no business in public office."

    @sherrodbrownoh.bsky.social on Bluesky, Sherrod Brown (official Bluesky account), August 21, 2026

  • Trade and tariffsSaid

    On October 11, 2025, writing about Ohio farmers and a U.S. financial package for Argentina, he called on Washington to change its tariff policies.

    "It’s time for Washington to fix its reckless tariff policies and do right by farmers here instead of in Argentina."

    @sherrodbrownoh.bsky.social on Bluesky, Sherrod Brown (official Bluesky account), October 11, 2025

Your script

Hi, my name is ____, and I'm a constituent from ____, ZIP ____.

I'm calling about AIPAC money, ICE and checks and balances.

I want my senator to refuse money from AIPAC PAC and United Democracy Project, and to say so publicly.

I want my senator to vote against more money for ICE detention and enforcement.

I want my senator to use Congress's oversight power, including hearings, subpoenas and votes, whichever party is in charge.

Please record my position. Thank you for your time.

Some offices log one issue per call. If they do, call back for the rest.

Call

    Tips for calling
    • Call your own senators and representative. Offices mostly count calls from their own voters.
    • Say your ZIP so the call counts as a constituent.
    • You'll talk to a staff member, not the official. That's normal: they log your call.
    • One or two issues per call is best.
    • Voicemail counts. Offices go through it every day.
    • If the D.C. office is busy, try a state office, or the U.S. Capitol Switchboard at (202) 224-3121.
    • Call again next week.
